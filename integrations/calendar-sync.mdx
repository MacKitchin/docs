Calendar sync removes the friction of keeping your team informed about upcoming meetings. When you schedule a meeting in ConnectMeetings, it appears in every attendee's Outlook calendar immediately — and stays accurate as details change, agendas are published, or meetings are moved or cancelled.

<Note>
  Calendar sync requires the Microsoft 365 integration to be connected. See [Connect ConnectMeetings to Microsoft 365](/integrations/microsoft-365) to get started.
</Note>

## How Sync Works

When a meeting is scheduled in ConnectMeetings, the platform creates a calendar event in each attendee's Outlook calendar on their behalf. The event is linked to the ConnectMeetings meeting record so that any subsequent changes are reflected automatically — no manual updates needed on either side.

Each attendee must have connected their personal Microsoft 365 account for the sync to reach their calendar. See [Per-User Calendar Connection](/integrations/microsoft-365#per-user-calendar-connection) for instructions.

## What Triggers a Sync

ConnectMeetings pushes an update to Outlook whenever any of the following events occur:

| Trigger | What Happens in Outlook |
|---|---|
| **Meeting created** | A new calendar event is created for all attendees. |
| **Meeting time changed** | The existing event is updated with the new date, time, and duration. |
| **Meeting cancelled** | The calendar event is automatically removed from all attendees' calendars. |
| **Agenda published** | The event description is updated with a direct link to the published agenda. |

## Calendar Event Contents

Each synced calendar event includes the following details:

- **Title** — the meeting name as entered in ConnectMeetings
- **Date and time** — start time and scheduled duration
- **Location** — physical address for in-person meetings, or the Microsoft Teams meeting link for virtual sessions
- **Agenda link** — a direct link to the published agenda in ConnectMeetings (added when the agenda is published)
- **Organizer contact** — name and email address of the meeting organizer

<Tip>
  The agenda link in the calendar event always points to the latest published version. Attendees never need to search for the most current agenda — it is one click away from their calendar.
</Tip>

## Two-Way Sync

Calendar sync is not one-directional. When an attendee responds to a meeting invite in Outlook — accepting, tentatively accepting, or declining — that response is reflected in the ConnectMeetings attendance tracker.

This means you can see expected attendance directly in ConnectMeetings without asking team members to confirm separately.

## Meeting Cancellations

When you cancel a meeting in ConnectMeetings, the corresponding Outlook calendar event is automatically deleted for all attendees. Attendees also receive a cancellation notification through Teams (if Teams notifications are enabled).

You do not need to send a separate cancellation from Outlook.

## Troubleshooting Sync Issues

If calendar events stop appearing or updating in Outlook, work through the following steps:

1. **Check the Microsoft 365 connection status.** Go to **Profile → Connected Accounts** and confirm your Microsoft account shows as connected. If it shows an error, click **Re-authorize** and sign in again.
2. **Confirm calendar write permission was granted.** During authorization, the read/write calendar permission must be accepted. If it was skipped, disconnect and reconnect the account to re-trigger the permission request.
3. **Check for tenant policy restrictions.** Some Microsoft 365 tenant policies restrict third-party calendar access. Ask your Microsoft 365 admin to confirm that ConnectMeetings is permitted under your organization's app consent policy.
4. **Contact support.** If the issue persists after re-authorizing, contact [ConnectMeetings support](mailto:support@connectmeetings.com) and include your organization name and affected user's email.
