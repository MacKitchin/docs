ConnectMeetings offers a deep, first-class integration with Microsoft 365. Once connected, meetings flow directly into your team's Outlook calendars, attendees receive Teams notifications at the right moments, and every agenda and minutes document is automatically archived in SharePoint — all without any manual file management.

## What You Get

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Outlook Calendar Sync" icon="calendar">
    Every scheduled meeting appears in attendees' Outlook calendars automatically. Updates and cancellations sync in real time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Teams Notifications" icon="bell">
    Attendees receive meeting reminders, agenda-published alerts, and action item assignments directly in Microsoft Teams.
  </Card>
  <Card title="SharePoint Document Storage" icon="folder-open">
    Agendas, minutes, and attachments are saved to a SharePoint site of your choice — organized by meeting date and committee.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Prerequisites

Before connecting, confirm you have the following:

- A **Microsoft 365 Business Basic** subscription or higher (Microsoft 365 Personal and @outlook.com accounts are not supported)
- **Global Admin** rights in your Microsoft tenant, or a delegated admin who can authorize third-party app permissions
- Admin access to your ConnectMeetings organization account

## Connecting the Integration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integration Settings">
    In ConnectMeetings, navigate to **Settings → Integrations → Microsoft 365**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the Connection">
    Click **Connect to Microsoft 365**. You will be redirected to the Microsoft sign-in page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign In with Your Work Account">
    Sign in with your Microsoft work account — the account associated with your organization's Microsoft 365 tenant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and Grant Permissions">
    Microsoft will display the list of permissions ConnectMeetings is requesting. Review them carefully, then click **Accept** to grant access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Connection">
    You are redirected back to ConnectMeetings. A green confirmation banner confirms the connection is active. Your organization's calendar is now syncing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Permissions Requested

ConnectMeetings requests the minimum permissions required to deliver the integration. The table below explains exactly what each permission is used for.

| Permission | Why ConnectMeetings Needs It |
|---|---|
| **Read/write calendar** | Creates, updates, and removes meeting events in attendees' Outlook calendars. |
| **Read user profile** | Reads each user's name and email address to correctly populate meeting invites. |
| **Send notifications** | Delivers meeting reminders and agenda alerts via Microsoft Teams. |

ConnectMeetings never reads the content of emails, personal files, or calendar events unrelated to meetings it manages.

## Per-User Calendar Connection

The organization-level connection above enables SharePoint storage and Teams notifications for your whole team. However, **calendar sync is per-user** — each team member must connect their own Microsoft account to allow ConnectMeetings to write events to their personal Outlook calendar.

Users can do this from **Profile → Connected Accounts → Connect Microsoft 365**.

<Note>
  Each user must individually authorize their own Microsoft account. Completing the organization-level setup does not automatically sync calendars for every member of your team.
</Note>

## SharePoint Document Storage

You can designate a SharePoint site where ConnectMeetings will automatically store all meeting documents.

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Microsoft 365 → SharePoint**.
2. Enter the URL of the SharePoint site you want to use.
3. Click **Authorize SharePoint Access** and sign in if prompted.
4. Choose or create a document library to use as the root storage folder.

Once configured, agendas and finalized minutes are uploaded automatically when they are published. Files are organized into folders by year, committee, and meeting date.

## Teams Notifications

Teams notifications are enabled by default when you connect Microsoft 365. You can manage notification preferences at **Settings → Integrations → Microsoft 365 → Teams**.

The following events trigger a Teams notification:

- A new meeting is scheduled and you are listed as an attendee
- An agenda is published for an upcoming meeting
- An action item is assigned to you
- A meeting is rescheduled or cancelled

Individual users can also adjust their own Teams notification preferences from **Profile → Notification Settings**.

<Warning>
  Microsoft 365 Business Basic or higher is required for this integration. Personal Microsoft accounts (such as @outlook.com or @hotmail.com addresses) are not supported and cannot be used to authorize the ConnectMeetings integration.
</Warning>
