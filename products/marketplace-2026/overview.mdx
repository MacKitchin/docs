## Availability legend

Many items in the 2026 kit are marked **SOLD** or **SOLD OUT**. Treat those flags as of the PDF export date:

- **SOLD / SOLD OUT** — not available unless inventory reopens; do not quote as open without sales confirmation
- **Per track** — Association, Corporate, International, Specialty, Sports, and sometimes Business Travel sell separately
- **Per day** — Day 1 / Day 2 / Day 3 inventory (emails, shirts, Amp-Up, Vibe, Safari Selfies, etc.)
