## Packages

### Custom video

Promote executive thought leadership, expertise, or unique offerings via dynamic video. Produced with the Connect video team for a captive audience of industry leaders — interviews, product demos, behind-the-scenes features.

**Pricing:** Confirm with sales (kit positions package without a fixed price on this page).

### Lead insights

Near real-time / actionable insights on attendee behavior and preferences to prioritize follow-up, personalize outreach, and improve ROI after the show.

**Pricing:** Confirm with sales.

### Digital media

Multi-channel package (social, email, display) to reach the target audience before, during, and after the trade show — product launches, promotions, and continuous visibility across the event cycle.

**Pricing:** Confirm with sales.

### Event targeting

Pixel-based capture of attendees entering the convention center; targeted ads during and after the event to reinforce messaging and extend campaign life beyond the show floor.

**Pricing:** Confirm with sales.
