## When to recommend each

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Pre/post-show emails">
    Best for exhibitors and sponsors who already have a show presence and need inbox continuity. Pair with Marketplace [dedicated trade show emails](/products/marketplace-2026/digital-sponsorships) when the event is Connect Marketplace specifically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Event targeting">
    Use when the goal is measurable click-based reach around a physical event, not just brand association. Kit pricing is performance-framed (400 clicks).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Lead insights">
    Recommend after campaigns or shows that generate leads — turns attendance and digital activity into follow-up prioritization. Lowest starting price in this category; strong attach product.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Custom video">
    Interviews, product demos, behind-the-scenes, and event-participation spotlight content marketed to industry audiences. Overlaps thematically with Marketplace event amplification packages.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
