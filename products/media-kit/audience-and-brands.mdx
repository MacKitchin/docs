## Network positioning

BizBash, TSNN, and Corporate Event News cover the meetings, events, and trade show industry as a single media network. Combined claims from the 2025 kit:

| Metric | Network total |
| --- | --: |
| Unique visits / month | 164K |
| Monthly views (owned) | 411K |
| Social audience (combined) | 378K |
| Newsletter subscribers | 93.8K |
| Average event budget | \$1.1M |

### TradeShow News Network (TSNN)

Go-to resource for **trade show professionals** — global ecosystem news, insights, and industry decision-makers (including C-level readers of _The Industry This Week_).

TSNN metrics on the brand spread are shown alongside the network; dedicated eblast inventory uses the **13K** TSNN database (see [Digital solutions](/products/media-kit/digital-solutions)).

## How to use these numbers

- Lead proposals with **network totals** when selling multi-brand packages.
- Use **brand-level uniques/views** when the buy is single-site (BizBash vs TSNN vs CEN).
- Pair trust and budget claims with product inclusions — do not invent ROI guarantees beyond what the kit states.
