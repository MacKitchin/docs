Public-sector organizations carry a high responsibility to protect the data they handle — from deliberation records to personal information about staff and constituents. ConnectMeetings is built with that responsibility in mind. We maintain rigorous security controls, pursue independent third-party certification, and offer data residency options designed for the compliance requirements of Canadian government and public-sector customers.

## Encryption

ConnectMeetings encrypts your data at every stage of its lifecycle.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="At Rest: AES-256" icon="lock">
    All stored data — meeting records, documents, minutes, and user data — is encrypted using AES-256, the industry standard for data-at-rest encryption.
  </Card>
  <Card title="In Transit: TLS 1.3" icon="shield-halved">
    All data transmitted between your browser or app and ConnectMeetings servers is protected by TLS 1.3. Older, less secure protocol versions are not accepted.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## SOC 2 Type II Certification

SOC 2 Type II is an independent audit that verifies a company's security controls are not only well-designed, but are operating effectively over time — typically a 6–12 month observation period. It covers five trust service principles: security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy.

ConnectMeetings is SOC 2 Type II certified. Our audit is conducted annually by an independent third-party auditor. This certification demonstrates that our security controls have been rigorously examined and verified — not just documented.

To request a copy of our most recent SOC 2 Type II report for your vendor assessment process, contact your account manager or email [security@connectmeetings.com](mailto:security@connectmeetings.com).

<Tip>
  Many public-sector procurement processes require vendors to provide a SOC 2 report as part of the security review. You can request our current SOC 2 Type II report at any point during your assessment. We typically respond within one business day.
</Tip>

## Data Residency

<Note>
  Canadian data residency is available on the **Enterprise** plan. Contact [sales@connectmeetings.com](mailto:sales@connectmeetings.com) to discuss upgrading.
</Note>

Enterprise customers can elect to have all of their organization's data stored exclusively in **Canadian data centers**. When Canadian data residency is enabled:

- All meeting records, documents, and user data are stored and processed in Canada
- No data is replicated to servers outside Canada
- Backups are also stored within Canada

This option is well-suited for organizations subject to **PIPEDA** (Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act) or provincial privacy legislation that requires personal information to remain within Canada.

To enable Canadian data residency for your organization, contact your account manager before your account is provisioned. Migrating an existing account to Canadian data residency requires coordination with our team.

## Access Controls

ConnectMeetings gives you precise control over what each person in your organization can see and do.

- **Roles** — Admins, Organizers, Members, and Viewers each have distinct permission levels. See [Roles and Permissions](/concepts/roles-and-permissions) for the full breakdown.
- **Audit logs** — every user action in ConnectMeetings is logged, including logins, document access, edits, and permission changes. Audit logs are available to Admins at **Settings → Security → Audit Log** and are retained for 12 months.

## Backups and Recovery

ConnectMeetings performs automated daily backups of all customer data. Our backup infrastructure supports:

- **Point-in-time recovery** — we can restore your organization's data to any point within the past 30 days
- **Geographic redundancy** — backups are stored in a separate data center from primary data to protect against facility-level failures

In the event that you need a data restore, contact [support@connectmeetings.com](mailto:support@connectmeetings.com) with your organization name and the target restore point.
