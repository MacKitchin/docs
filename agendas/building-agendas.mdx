Every meeting in ConnectMeetings has a dedicated agenda that keeps participants informed and discussions on track. You build the agenda directly inside the meeting record, attach supporting documents, and distribute it to all attendees with a single click once it is approved.

## Creating an agenda

Open any meeting and select the **Agenda** tab. ConnectMeetings automatically creates a blank agenda linked to that meeting — there is nothing extra to configure. You can begin adding items immediately.

## Adding agenda items

Click **Add Item** to insert a new agenda item. Each item supports the following fields:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Short, descriptive name for the item |
| **Type** | Category that signals how the item will be handled |
| **Presenter** | Person responsible for leading that portion of the meeting |
| **Time Allocation** | Estimated minutes reserved for the item |
| **Description** | Background context, talking points, or instructions |
| **Attachments** | Supporting files participants can review in advance |

### Item types

ConnectMeetings provides five built-in item types to match common governance formats:

| Type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Information** | Presented for awareness; no vote required |
| **Action** | Requires a motion, discussion, and vote |
| **Presentation** | Allocated time for a formal presentation |
| **Consent** | Grouped items approved together without separate debate |
| **Public Comment** | Designated time for public input |

## Ordering items

Drag any agenda item up or down to reorder it. ConnectMeetings renumbers all items automatically whenever the order changes. Consistent numbering flows through to the published agenda and the minutes record.

## Attachments

Select an item and click **Attach File** to upload supporting documents. Accepted formats include PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), and PowerPoint (.pptx). All attachments are stored securely and made available to every invited attendee when the agenda is published.

<Tip>
Upload attachments before publishing the agenda so participants can review materials in advance. Attachments added after publishing are still accessible, but attendees will not receive a second notification.
</Tip>

## Saving and previewing

Click **Save Draft** at any time to preserve your work without publishing. Use the **Preview** button to see exactly how the agenda will appear to participants — including item numbers, presenter names, time allocations, and attachment links. Preview the agenda before submitting it for approval to catch any errors early.

## Publishing and distributing

Once the agenda is approved, click **Publish**. ConnectMeetings immediately:

- Sends an email notification to all invited attendees with a link to the agenda
- Updates the calendar event in Microsoft 365 with the agenda details
- Makes all attachments accessible through the meeting portal

<Note>
Once an agenda is approved and published, it is locked for editing. Any changes after approval require the agenda to be resubmitted through the approval workflow before it can be republished. This protects the integrity of the officially distributed version.
</Note>

## Full agenda creation flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the meeting">
    Navigate to **Meetings**, select your meeting, and click the **Agenda** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add agenda items">
    Click **Add Item** for each topic. Set the title, type, presenter, time allocation, and description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach supporting documents">
    Select each item and upload relevant PDFs, Word docs, or spreadsheets using **Attach File**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reorder items">
    Drag items into the correct sequence. Confirm the automatic numbering looks correct.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and preview">
    Click **Save Draft**, then **Preview** to review the agenda from a participant's perspective.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for approval">
    Click **Submit for Approval** to send the agenda through your configured approval workflow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish once approved">
    After approval, click **Publish** to distribute the agenda to all attendees by email and calendar update.
  </Step>
</Steps>
