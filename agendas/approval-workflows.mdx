Publishing an agenda with an error — a missing item, an incorrect presenter, or an attachment that was not cleared for release — can undermine trust in a formal governance process. Approval workflows give designated reviewers a chance to verify the agenda before it reaches attendees, creating a clear paper trail of who reviewed and approved it.

<Note>
Agenda approval workflows are available on the **Professional plan** and above. Organizations on the Starter plan can publish agendas directly without a formal approval step.
</Note>

## Why approval workflows matter

In formal governance settings — city councils, school boards, and public commissions — agendas often carry legal significance. They define what a body is permitted to discuss and vote on in a given session. A workflow ensures:

- The right people review the agenda before it is distributed
- Errors are caught and corrected before participants rely on the document
- A timestamped approval record is retained for audit purposes

## Workflow stages

Every agenda in an approval workflow moves through the following stages:

| Stage | Description |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Organizer is building the agenda; not yet submitted for review |
| **Submitted for Review** | Organizer has submitted; approvers are notified and reviewing |
| **Changes Requested** | An approver has returned the agenda with revision comments; organizer can edit and resubmit |
| **Rejected** | An approver has declined the agenda; it cannot be resubmitted without starting a new draft |
| **Approved** | All required approvers have signed off; agenda is ready to publish |
| **Distributed** | Agenda has been published and sent to attendees |

## Configuring approvers

Set up approvers at the meeting level or as a default for a meeting series.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Agenda Approval settings">
    Open the meeting and go to **Meeting Settings → Agenda Approval**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add approvers">
    Click **Add Approver** and search for each person by name or email. Repeat until all required approvers are listed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose approval mode">
    Select the mode that matches your review chain:
    - **Sequential** — approvers review one at a time in the order you specify
    - **Parallel** — all approvers review simultaneously; the agenda advances once everyone has approved
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Use sequential approval for clerk → chair sign-off chains. For example, the City Clerk reviews the agenda for completeness first, and once approved, it moves to the Mayor or Chair for final sign-off. This mirrors the real-world review hierarchy most governance bodies already follow.
</Tip>

## Submitting for review

When the agenda is ready, click **Submit for Approval** from the Agenda tab. ConnectMeetings:

- Locks the agenda so it cannot be edited while under review
- Sends an email notification to the first approver (sequential) or all approvers (parallel) with a direct link to the agenda
- Updates the agenda status to **Submitted for Review**

## Reviewing and approving

Approvers receive an email with a link to the agenda review page. On that page, an approver can:

- **Browse all agenda items and attachments** in the same view participants will see
- **Add comments** on specific items or the agenda overall
- **Approve** — moves the agenda to the next approver or marks it fully approved
- **Request Changes** — returns the agenda to the organizer with comments, unlocking it for editing

All comments and decisions are time-stamped and stored in the agenda's activity log.

## Re-submission after changes

If an approver requests changes, the agenda status returns to **Draft** and the organizer is notified by email. The organizer can view the approver's comments directly on the agenda, make the necessary edits, and then click **Resubmit for Approval**. The review cycle restarts from the beginning of the approver chain.

## Bypassing approval

Admins can approve and publish an agenda directly without going through the normal workflow. This is intended for urgent or emergency meetings where there is not enough time for the standard review process.

To bypass approval, open the meeting as an Admin, go to the Agenda tab, and click **Admin Approve & Publish**.

<Note>
Bypassed approvals are flagged in the agenda's activity log as "Admin override" with a timestamp and the Admin's name. This maintains auditability even when the normal workflow is skipped.
</Note>

## Full approval workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build the agenda">
    Complete the agenda draft — add all items, presenters, time allocations, and attachments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure approvers">
    Go to **Meeting Settings → Agenda Approval**, add your approvers, and choose sequential or parallel mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for approval">
    Click **Submit for Approval**. The agenda locks and approvers are notified by email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approvers review">
    Each approver opens the agenda, reviews items and attachments, and either approves or requests changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revise if needed">
    If changes are requested, edit the agenda and click **Resubmit for Approval** to restart the review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish once approved">
    Once all approvers have signed off, click **Publish** to distribute the agenda to all attendees.
  </Step>
</Steps>
