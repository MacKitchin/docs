Effective governance requires more than running meetings — it requires understanding how well those meetings are working. ConnectMeetings analytics give you clear, data-driven visibility into attendance patterns, whether meetings stay on schedule, and how reliably action items get resolved. These insights help you drive accountability, improve efficiency, and demonstrate organizational performance to stakeholders.

<Note>
  Analytics are available on the **Professional** and **Enterprise** plans. Starter plan users can upgrade from **Settings → Plan** to gain access.
</Note>

## Accessing Analytics

Navigate to **Analytics** from the main left-hand menu. If you do not see the Analytics menu item, your plan may not include this feature.

## Meeting Metrics

### Attendance Rate

ConnectMeetings tracks attendance at three levels:

- **By meeting** — see which meetings had strong or poor turnout
- **By member** — identify individuals with low attendance patterns across meetings
- **By department** — compare participation rates across teams or committees

### Actual vs. Scheduled Duration

For every meeting, ConnectMeetings records the scheduled duration and the actual duration based on when the meeting was marked as started and ended. The analytics dashboard surfaces:

- Meetings that consistently run over their scheduled time
- The average overrun (in minutes) across your organization
- Trends over time showing whether meeting efficiency is improving

### Agenda Adherence

Agenda adherence measures what percentage of agenda items were completed within their allotted time during a meeting. A score of 100% means every item was addressed on schedule. This metric helps you identify:

- Meetings where planning and time allocation need adjustment
- Recurring agenda items that consistently run over time
- Committees or organizers who may benefit from facilitation support

## Action Item Metrics

Action item tracking gives you visibility into whether decisions made in meetings are actually being executed.

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| **Completion rate by assignee** | What percentage of action items each person completes on time. |
| **Completion rate by department** | Aggregate completion performance across teams or committees. |
| **Average days to complete** | How long it typically takes an item to move from assigned to resolved. |
| **Overdue item count** | The number of action items that have passed their due date without resolution. |

## Filtering and Date Ranges

All analytics views support the following filters. Apply them individually or in combination to focus on the data that matters most.

- **Date range** — select a preset (last 30 days, last quarter, year to date) or define a custom range
- **Committee** — filter by a specific committee or standing body
- **Organizer** — view metrics for meetings run by a particular person
- **Meeting type** — separate regular meetings from special sessions, public hearings, or workshops

## Exporting Reports

You can export any analytics view for use in external reports or presentations.

1. Apply the filters and date range you want to capture.
2. Click **Export** in the top-right corner of the analytics page.
3. Choose **CSV** for raw data (suitable for spreadsheet analysis) or **PDF** for formatted reports (suitable for board or council presentations).

The export reflects exactly what is visible in the current filtered view.

<Tip>
  Department-level analytics are particularly useful for budget reporting. Use the **By Department** attendance and duration views to calculate staff time invested in meetings, which can support cost-of-meeting reporting for annual budget reviews or audits.
</Tip>
