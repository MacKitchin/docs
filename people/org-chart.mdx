## Reading the structure

A few things that are not obvious from the boxes.

- **Sales is the largest function by far** and is split three ways: Suzi Schnell runs Connect marketplace sales, Michela Giovannotto runs BizBash, Andrew Dysart runs media and travel. Matthew Lee sits alongside them as Sr. Director of Sales.
- **Technology & AI reports into Sales**, not into a separate technology function. Mac Kitchin and Wei Zheng both sit under Andrew Dysart. This is why the AI roadmap is framed in commercial hours-saved terms.
- **Customer Success is distributed**, not a department. Its three managers report into different sales leaders: Orlon Daniels to Andrew Dysart, Kimberly Dorf to Suzi Schnell, Monica Garcia to Michela Giovannotto.
- **Editorial sits partly outside Marketing.** Sarah Kloepple reports directly to Matt Johnson, and Danica Tormohlen reports to Kimberly Collie, while the marketing team reports to Jordan Bouldin.
- **A meaningful share of content is contract**, with five freelance writers and contractors attached mostly to editorial and BizBash.
