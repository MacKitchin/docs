Finding a time that works for everyone is one of the biggest friction points in meeting coordination. ConnectMeetings smart scheduling solves this by reading availability directly from connected Microsoft 365 calendars and surfacing open slots that work for every required attendee — no more email chains, no more double-bookings.

## How Smart Scheduling Works

Smart scheduling checks each attendee's Microsoft 365 calendar and identifies overlapping free windows within your chosen date range. When you click **Find Available Times**, ConnectMeetings presents a ranked list of open slots based on your scheduling constraints. The suggestions refresh every time you open the scheduling panel, so you always see current availability.

<Note>
  Smart scheduling requires all attendees to have a connected Microsoft 365 calendar. Attendees who have not connected their calendar are excluded from availability checks, and their conflicts will not be reflected in the suggested slots.
</Note>

## Required vs. Optional Attendees

How you classify attendees directly affects which slots are surfaced.

- **Required attendees** — ConnectMeetings only suggests slots where every required attendee is free. A slot is hidden if even one required attendee has a conflict.
- **Optional attendees** — Their availability is shown as an overlay on each suggested slot, but it does not filter out options. You can see at a glance how many optional attendees are free for each slot.

Mark attendees as required or optional when you add them in the attendee panel. You can change the designation at any time before the meeting is confirmed.

## Creating a Meeting with Smart Scheduling

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a New Meeting">
    From your dashboard, click **New Meeting** in the top navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Smart Scheduling">
    In the meeting setup panel, toggle **Smart Scheduling** to the on position. The manual date and time fields are replaced by the scheduling assistant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Required Attendees">
    In the **Attendees** field, search for and add each participant. Set their role to **Required** or **Optional** using the dropdown next to each name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Duration and Date Range">
    Choose the meeting **Duration** (e.g., 30 min, 1 hour) and a **Date Range** for the search window (e.g., the next 5 business days). Apply any scheduling constraints as needed — see the section below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View Suggested Slots">
    Click **Find Available Times**. ConnectMeetings displays a ranked list of open slots, with an attendance summary for each option showing how many optional attendees are also free.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Slot">
    Click the slot that works best. A preview of the meeting details appears on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Meeting">
    Review the details and click **Confirm Meeting**. Invitations are sent automatically to all attendees.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Scheduling Constraints

You can narrow the search window using constraints in the scheduling assistant panel.

| Constraint | Description |
|---|---|
| **Working hours only** | Limits suggestions to each attendee's defined working hours. Defaults to 9 AM – 5 PM if not set on the attendee's profile. |
| **Buffer time** | Adds a gap before and/or after the meeting. Useful for travel time or preparation. Options are 5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes. |
| **Preferred days** | Restrict suggestions to specific days of the week (e.g., Tuesdays and Thursdays only). |
| **Preferred time of day** | Bias results toward mornings, middays, or afternoons without excluding other times entirely. |

Set constraints before clicking **Find Available Times**. Changing a constraint automatically refreshes the suggestions list.

<Tip>
  For urgent meetings that need to happen as soon as possible, click **Earliest Available** instead of browsing the full list. ConnectMeetings immediately books the first open slot that satisfies all constraints and sends invitations right away.
</Tip>
