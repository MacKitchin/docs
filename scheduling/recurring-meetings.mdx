Standing committees, city councils, and school boards meet on a predictable schedule — weekly, monthly, or quarterly. ConnectMeetings recurring meetings let you define that schedule once and automatically generate every occurrence, complete with a shared agenda template, consistent attendee list, and pre-configured reminders. You spend less time on logistics and more time on the work that matters.

## Creating a Recurring Series

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a New Meeting">
    From your dashboard, click **New Meeting** in the top navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Recurring">
    In the meeting setup panel, toggle **Recurring** to the on position. A recurrence configuration panel appears below the date and time fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Recurrence Pattern">
    Choose one of the available patterns: **Weekly**, **Bi-weekly**, **Monthly**, or **Custom**. Configure the pattern details — for example, which days of the week, or which date of the month. See the options below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Series End">
    Choose when the series ends: after a specific **number of occurrences**, on a specific **end date**, or select **No end date** for an ongoing series. You can end the series at any time from the series management view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Attendees and Details">
    Add your attendees, location or video link, and any other meeting details. These apply to the entire series but can be overridden on individual occurrences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach an Agenda Template (Optional)">
    In the **Agenda** section, click **Attach Template** and choose an existing template. Each new occurrence will automatically start with that template's structure. See the section below for more details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the Series">
    Click **Create Series**. ConnectMeetings generates all scheduled occurrences and sends invitations to all attendees.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recurrence Options

ConnectMeetings supports the following recurrence patterns.

| Pattern | Configuration Options |
|---|---|
| **Weekly** | One or more days of the week (e.g., every Tuesday and Thursday) |
| **Bi-weekly** | Every two weeks on selected days |
| **Monthly (by date)** | A specific date each month (e.g., the 15th) |
| **Monthly (by day)** | A specific day-of-week occurrence (e.g., first Monday, last Friday) |
| **Custom interval** | Any combination of weeks or months (e.g., every 6 weeks) |

For monthly patterns, use **by day-of-week** when the meeting should always fall on a business day regardless of how the calendar falls (e.g., "first Monday of every month" rather than "the 1st").

## Series vs. Individual Occurrence

You can edit a recurring series at two levels: the entire series or a single occurrence.

- **Edit entire series** — Changes apply to all future occurrences from today onward. Past occurrences are not affected. Use this when the meeting time, attendees, or recurrence pattern changes permanently.
- **Edit single occurrence** — Changes apply only to that specific meeting date. The rest of the series remains unchanged. Use this for one-off exceptions such as a location change or a guest attendee.

<Warning>
  When you open an occurrence and click **Edit**, ConnectMeetings asks whether you want to edit **this occurrence** or **this and all future occurrences**. Choose carefully — selecting "all future occurrences" will overwrite any individual customizations you have already made to upcoming meetings in the series. This action cannot be undone.
</Warning>

## Agenda Templates on Recurring Meetings

Attaching an agenda template to a series ensures every occurrence starts with the same structure — standing agenda items, sections, and pre-filled descriptions — while still allowing each meeting's agenda to be customized before publication.

When a new occurrence is created, ConnectMeetings copies the template into the occurrence as a draft agenda. Changes you make to that occurrence's agenda do not affect the template or other occurrences.

<Tip>
  If your committee has a standard agenda structure (e.g., Call to Order, Approval of Minutes, Committee Reports, New Business, Adjournment), build it once as an **Agenda Template** under **Settings > Agenda Templates**, then attach it to the series. Every new occurrence will be pre-populated and ready to customize in seconds.
</Tip>

To update the template attached to a series, go to the series detail view, click **Edit Series**, and choose a different template from the **Agenda Template** dropdown. Future occurrences will use the new template; past and already-customized occurrences are not affected.

## Managing a Series

You can view and manage all occurrences in a series from the **Series View**.

**To open the Series View**, click the series name from your meeting list and select **View All Occurrences**. This shows a timeline of every past, upcoming, and cancelled occurrence.

### Cancelling a Single Occurrence

Open the occurrence you want to cancel, click **Cancel Occurrence**, and enter an optional cancellation reason. Attendees receive a cancellation notification automatically. The occurrence remains visible in the Series View with a **Cancelled** label.

### Ending a Series

To stop all future occurrences, open the Series View and click **End Series**. Choose an effective date — occurrences scheduled after that date are cancelled and attendees are notified. Occurrences before the effective date are preserved with their existing data.

### Reactivating or Editing a Cancelled Occurrence

A cancelled single occurrence can be reinstated. Open the cancelled occurrence in the Series View and click **Reinstate Occurrence**. Update the date or time if needed and save — invitations are re-sent to all attendees.
