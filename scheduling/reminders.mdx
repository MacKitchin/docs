Keeping every participant informed at the right moment reduces no-shows, ensures action items get done, and makes your meetings run smoothly. ConnectMeetings sends automated email and SMS reminders at key points in the meeting lifecycle — from agenda publication through post-meeting follow-up — so nothing falls through the cracks.

## Reminder Types

ConnectMeetings sends reminders for four distinct trigger points.

### Agenda Published

This reminder is sent when you publish an agenda and distribute it to attendees. It includes a link to view the full agenda and any attached documents. Attendees receive this notification as soon as you click **Publish Agenda**.

### Pre-Meeting Reminders

These reminders are sent before the meeting starts. The default schedule is:

- **24 hours before** — Includes the agenda link, dial-in or location details, and a list of pre-read materials.
- **1 hour before** — A shorter nudge with the meeting link and time.
- **15 minutes before** — A final prompt with a direct join link.

All three intervals are enabled by default. You can disable individual intervals or adjust their timing at the organization level or per meeting.

### Post-Meeting Notifications

After a meeting concludes, ConnectMeetings sends two follow-up notifications:

- **Minutes available** — Sent when the meeting minutes are published. Includes a link to review and approve the draft.
- **Action items assigned** — Sent to each attendee who has been assigned one or more action items. Lists the item description, due date, and a link to the action item tracker.

### Action Item Due Date Reminders

When an action item has a due date, the assignee receives a reminder **48 hours before** the deadline and a second reminder **on the due date** if the item is still open. These reminders continue until the item is marked complete.

## Where to Configure Reminders

You can manage reminders at two levels.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Organization Defaults" icon="building">
    Go to **Settings > Notifications** to configure default reminder behavior for all meetings in your organization. Changes here apply to any new meeting created going forward.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Per-Meeting Settings" icon="calendar">
    Open any meeting and select the **Reminders** tab. Override the org defaults for that specific meeting without affecting other meetings.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Configure your organization defaults in **Settings > Notifications** before creating meetings. This way, every meeting automatically inherits the right reminder schedule and you only need to make per-meeting changes for exceptions.
</Tip>

## Email Reminders

Email reminders are available on all plans and are enabled by default. Each email includes:

- Meeting name, date, time, and location or video link
- A link to the agenda (if published)
- A personalized greeting using the recipient's display name

Emails are sent from `notifications@connectmeetings.io` with your organization's name in the sender display field. You can customize the email footer text under **Settings > Notifications > Email Branding**.

All confirmed attendees, required and optional, receive email reminders unless they have opted out of that reminder type in their profile.

## SMS Reminders

<Note>
  SMS reminders are available on the **Enterprise plan** only. If your organization is on a Starter or Professional plan, the SMS option will not appear in notification settings.
</Note>

SMS reminders send a short text message to attendees before a meeting. To receive SMS reminders, an attendee must have a verified mobile number saved on their user profile under **Profile > Contact Info**.

SMS reminders are sent for the **1-hour** and **15-minute** pre-meeting intervals by default. You can adjust this in **Settings > Notifications > SMS Timing**.

## Per-User Notification Preferences

Individual users can control which reminder types they receive. Each user can go to **Profile > Notification Preferences** and toggle off any reminder category — for example, opting out of action item due date reminders while keeping pre-meeting reminders active.

User-level preferences override org defaults but do not affect reminders sent to other attendees. Administrators cannot force reminders on users who have opted out.

## Reminder Reference Table

| Reminder Type | Trigger | Default Timing | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agenda published | Agenda is distributed | Immediately | Email |
| Pre-meeting (long) | Before meeting start | 24 hours before | Email |
| Pre-meeting (medium) | Before meeting start | 1 hour before | Email, SMS* |
| Pre-meeting (short) | Before meeting start | 15 minutes before | Email, SMS* |
| Minutes available | Minutes are published | Immediately | Email |
| Action items assigned | Minutes are published | Immediately | Email |
| Action item due (early) | Before item due date | 48 hours before | Email |
| Action item due (day-of) | On item due date | 8 AM on due date | Email |

*SMS available on Enterprise plan only, and only when the user has a verified mobile number on their profile.
