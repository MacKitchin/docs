## Staff guidance

Read [Staff and agent guardrails](/operations/ai/guardrails) first, then [Agent retrieval rules](/operations/ai/agent-retrieval). Inventory is on the [agent roster](/operations/ai/agents). Tooling is on [MCP and tooling](/operations/ai/tooling).

## Customer-safe information

Approved FAQ short answers and published event or package facts, only after the agent has retrieved the page and checked When-not-to-use rules.

## Internal-only context

Agent configuration, MCP servers, Elysia credentials locations, prompt libraries, and source confidence scores.

## Limits and escalation

Agents must retrieve before answering, cite the page, and stop at billing, legal, contracts, complaints, discounts, and unpublished rates. Those go to a human owner.
