This page is an internal extract of the Notion **Customer FAQs** database — the Canonical home for approved response pairs. Staff and customer-facing agents should use the **Short answer** field as customer-safe wording, then check the [Event calendar](/events/calendar/overview) for dates, venues, and policy. Do not rewrite the answers from memory or share the internal annotations.

<Info>
  **Source:** Notion database `🙋‍♂️ Customer FAQs` under the Salesforce Knowledge Base. **Owner:** Mac Kitchin. **Refresh:** monthly. **Extracted:** 12 August 2026. **Last validated in source:** 26 June 2026. Registry notes say ~31 pairs; this extract found **29** rows. Every row is still status **In progress**.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Several answers mention billing, discounts, payment timing, or attendee lists. Those rows carry a **When not to use** rule. Follow it. Escalate amounts, terms, and named lists to a human — see [Guardrails](/operations/ai/guardrails).
</Warning>

Event facts in this library are restated from email and onboarding copy. If a pair disagrees with the [Event calendar](/events/calendar/overview) or [Known issues](/reference/known-issues), report both internally. Do not pick a winner.

## How to use a pair

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Short answer** | The approved reply. Quote this, do not paraphrase into a new policy. |
| **Confidence** | Source-scored. Surface the score when it is below 0.8, or when the field is blank. |
| **Frequency / rank** | How often the question appeared in the mined corpus. Blank means unranked. |
| **When not to use** | Hard stop. Usually account-specific money or PII. |

Sensitive review-queue items (specific discounts, invoice schedules, unpublished rates) stay in Knowledge Candidates. They are not on this page.

## Hosted buyer and attendee

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My application is approved — what are the next steps for Connect Marketplace?">
    **Short answer:** Add the dates (Aug 24-26, Tampa), book your hotel via the link, watch for your flight invite from TripEasy, and complete your appointment-portal selections. Marketplace 1:1 appointments are the only mandatory requirement (8/25 10am - 8/26 5pm).

    **When to use:** Newly approved/secured attendee asking "what now?"

    Frequency 30 · Rank 5 · Confidence 0.85
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How and when do I get my deposit back?">
    **Short answer:** You receive a refund check at the conclusion of your 1:1 appointments — but you must attend ALL of your scheduled 1:1 appointments to receive your deposit back.

    **When to use:** Attendee asking about deposit refund timing or conditions.

    Frequency 14 · Rank 14 · Confidence 0.85

    <Note>
      The [Hosted Buyer application playbook](/hosted-buyer-support/application-and-approval) and the archived VIP playbook also describe an on-site or post-completion refund. Mechanism and timing remain unconfirmed. Report both versions internally and do not invent a single process.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who books my flight and how?">
    **Short answer:** A flight-booking invitation comes directly from our travel partner, TripEasy (`travelnoreply@tripeasy.com`) — add it to your safe-sender list. The travel team (`connectmeetings@its.com`) can help.

    **When to use:** Attendee asking how/when to book flights or who to contact for travel.

    Frequency 12 · Rank 18 · Confidence 0.82
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many appointments will I have, and how do I choose who I meet?">
    **Short answer:** You'll meet roughly 30-40 suppliers/buyers over the event. Closer to the date you'll get appointment-portal access to make your selections — make as many as possible.

    **When to use:** Attendee asking how many meetings or how matchmaking works.

    Frequency 12 · Rank 17 · Confidence 0.80
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a referral program / can I refer a colleague?">
    **Short answer:** Yes — "Connect is Better with Friends". Refer a qualified planner who has never attended a Connect event; if they're approved and attend, you both get \$250 after the event.

    **When to use:** Attendee asking about referrals or incentives for bringing peers.

    Frequency 8 · Confidence 0.80
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I transfer my hosted invite or registration to a colleague?">
    **Short answer:** Yes, with rep approval and if the colleague is qualified (typically director-level or above).

    **When to use:** Invitee declines but wants to send a colleague.

    Confidence blank in the database (source note 0.70).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the Hosted Buyer / Fully Hosted VIP program and am I eligible?">
    **Short answer:** Select director-level+ buyers (e.g., DMO/state tourism leaders) are fully hosted — flight and hotel covered — in exchange for taking pre-scheduled appointments. A refundable deposit secures the spot.

    **When to use:** Buyer/DMO asking about hosted invitations, eligibility, or what's covered.

    **When not to use:** Pricing/financial specifics beyond the refundable deposit — confirm with the rep.

    Confidence blank (source note 0.80).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is eTourism Marketplace and how does hosting work?">
    **Short answer:** An intimate destination-marketing show (Nov 18-20, 2026, West Palm Beach) with max 75 hosted DMO/state leaders and 75 travel suppliers, built around 35 pre-scheduled 1:1 appointments.

    **When to use:** DMO/supplier asking about eTourism Marketplace format, dates, or hosting.

    <Warning>

## Supplier packages, appointments, and events

<AccordionGroup>

## Media, creative, and campaigns

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="What print/editorial advertising is available and what are the deadlines?">

## Contracting, payment, and discounts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I sign up — what is the contract process?">
    **Short answer:** Agreements are sent and signed via DocuSign; once signed, the campaign/participation is secured and fulfillment begins.

    **When to use:** Supplier ready to move forward or asking how to commit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I pay my invoice and what payment methods are accepted?">
    **Short answer:** Your Customer Success Manager sends an invoice with due dates; pay via our secure, fee-free credit-card link ("BizBashPayNow", powered by Square). Large agreements are commonly billed in quarterly installments.

    **When to use:** Supplier asking how to pay, payment methods, or installment options.

    **When not to use:** Specific amounts/schedules — account-specific and sensitive.

    Frequency 20 · Rank 8 · Confidence 0.80
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When is payment due after I sign an agreement?">
    **Short answer:** Payment terms are set in your agreement; reps send "Payment Due" reminders and some packages require a deposit. Specific amounts/terms vary — confirm on your agreement.

    **When to use:** Supplier asking about payment timing or terms.

    **When not to use:** Do not quote specific amounts here — account-specific and sensitive.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I get a discount or reduce my package cost?">
    **Short answer:** Packages are custom and reps can offer tiered options, partnership discounts, or apply a credit on file — but specific reductions require rep/manager approval. Share your budget and goals and we'll tailor a package.

    **When to use:** Supplier asking to revisit pricing or fit within budget.

    **When not to use:** Do not quote specific discounts/amounts — account-specific and sensitive.

    Frequency 20 · Rank 9 · Confidence 0.70
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do existing partners get deals or discounts on other events?">
    **Short answer:** It's handled case-by-case; reps will take an active partnership into account when proposing participation in another event.

    **When to use:** Partner asking about cross-event discounts or bundles.

    **When not to use:** Promising a specific discount — this is discretionary and needs rep/manager input.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What incentives are offered to sign up early?">
    **Short answer:** Time-boxed incentives like waived fees, gift cards, sweepstakes (e.g., FIFA/World Cup tickets), and BOGO offers are used to drive early sign-ups before deadlines.

    **When to use:** Supplier asking about current promos or the deal if they sign now.

    **When not to use:** Don't promise expired promotions; confirm current offer with the rep.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
