## If you are a new hire

Read in this order. It should take about twenty minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Understand the business">
    [Who we are](/company/overview), then [Business model](/company/business-model), then [Brands](/company/brands). Nothing else makes sense until the supplier-and-hosted-buyer economy does.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn the calendar">
    [Event calendar](/events/overview). The event calendar drives almost every deadline in the business. Read this before the org chart.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your people">
    [Departments](/people/departments) is the map. [Who owns what](/people/who-owns-what) is the routing table. [Org chart](/people/org-chart) is reporting lines. [Directory](/people/directory) is emails and locations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn the vocabulary">
    [Glossary](/start/glossary). Terms like IO, hosted buyer, product year, and marketplace mean something specific here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Know where truth lives">
    [Source of truth](/governance/source-of-truth). Before you edit anything or answer anything, know which system owns that fact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn the approved customer answers">
    [Customer FAQs](/start/customer-faqs). Twenty-nine grounded pairs with confidence scores. Cite these for supplier and hosted-buyer questions; still take event dates from the calendar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Salesforce fields, Visit Create APIs, Agentforce, and Elysia live on the **Systems & agents** tab. Skip that tab until you need to query the org or build an agent. Agent retrieval rules are on [Agent retrieval rules](/start/agent-retrieval).
</Note>

## Conventions used here

| Convention | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Product year | The calendar year a product is sold *for*, not when the deal closed |
| Area vs project | Areas are ongoing departments; projects are time-bound and end |
| Canonical / Working / Deprecated | Truth levels from the source-of-truth registry |
| A note in square brackets | An unresolved discrepancy carried over from the source, deliberately not reconciled |

## What this site is not

It does not hold record-level data. No account lists, no contact details for customers, no deal values by name, no attendee rosters. Those live in Salesforce and are governed by access control there. This site documents structure, definitions, ownership, and process.
