## Before relying on an answer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the source">
    Use the [source-of-truth registry](/reference/source-of-truth) to identify the system that owns the fact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the status">
    **Canonical** material is preferred. **Working** material must be verified. **Deprecated** material must not be presented as current.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check for a known conflict">
    If two sources disagree, report both versions and route reconciliation to the source owner. Do not choose a winner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the communication boundary">
    Use the relevant support playbook to distinguish customer-safe information from internal-only context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Escalate when required">
    Billing, invoices, legal, contracts, complaints, specific discounts, and unpublished rates always require a human owner.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  This site documents structure, meaning, ownership, and process. Query the owning system for live record data; do not infer current customer or deal state from a Company Brain page.
