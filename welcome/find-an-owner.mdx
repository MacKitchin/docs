## Staff guidance

Use [Who owns what](/people/who-owns-what) for systems, datasets, and escalations. Use the [staff directory](/people/directory) for email addresses. Use [Departments](/people/departments) for area remit.

## Customer-safe information

Named public event or sales contacts only after checking [Known issues](/reference/known-issues). Do not present an internal owner as a customer-facing inbox unless the page says so.

## Internal-only context

Ownership gaps, dual recorded managers, and Finance/Customer Success source drift.

## Limits and escalation

Do not invent an owner to fill a gap. Customer Success remains unowned as an area. Billing goes to Stephen Han (Finance).
