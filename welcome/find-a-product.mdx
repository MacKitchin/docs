## Staff guidance

Start at [Products and services overview](/products-services/overview). For marketplace appointment packages use [Packages and published pricing](/exhibitor-support/packages-and-pricing). Confirm the source date before quoting.

## Customer-safe information

Published package contents and prices on the package tables, with the source date stated.

## Internal-only context

Unpublished sponsorship prospectus rates, availability counts that may be stale, and internal commercial strategy.

## Limits and escalation

Never quote a specific rate beyond published package tables. Never invent a discount or extend an expired offer. Discounts go to the account's sales owner. Billing and invoice amounts go to Stephen Han (Finance).
