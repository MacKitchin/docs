Most of the pipeline value from a Connect event is realised in the six to twelve weeks after you leave. Treat the marketplace as the start of the sales cycle, not the finish.

## Follow-up cadence

<Steps>
  <Step title="Within 48 hours">
    A personalised message to every buyer you met, referencing something specific from the appointment. Attach the digital pack and any custom offer you promised in the meeting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Two weeks after">
    Second-touch outreach on the buyers who did not respond. A shorter, more direct message on a specific date range or opportunity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="30 to 60 days after">
    Move active conversations into proposals and quotes. Update your CRM with opportunity stage and expected close date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Quarterly">
    Nurture the room. Buyers who did not book this year are pipeline for next year.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Lead handling

- **Consolidate** your appointment notes, walk-up conversations, and any leads captured through amplification products into a single source of truth
- **Score** based on fit and timing, not just enthusiasm. Buyers with defined dates and rooms on peak convert; interested browsers do not
- **Tag** the source event on every lead so you can measure event-level performance later
- **Route** into your CRM promptly. Leads that sit uncontacted for a week convert at a fraction of the rate

## Rebooking

If you did not rebook onsite in the [rebooking lounge](/exhibitors/onsite), do it in the first two weeks after the event. Two reasons:

1. Momentum from the onsite experience decays quickly and rebooking with fresh outcomes in mind is much easier
2. Popular tiers, especially Deal Closer at the flagship, cap at a small number of packages and can sell out

<Info>
  Across the Connect portfolio, renewals close at roughly **85%** at nearly **twice the average deal size** of net new business. Rebooking is the revenue engine.
</Info>

## Measuring return

A basic supplier ROI framework:

| Metric | How to measure |
| --- | --- |
| Cost per qualified conversation | Package price divided by qualified appointments (exclude no-shows and poor-fit matches) |
| Pipeline generated | Sum of opportunity value tagged to the event within 90 days |
| Closed-won revenue | Sum of closed-won opportunities tagged to the event within 12 months |
| Payback period | Months to recoup total event spend, including travel and amplification |
