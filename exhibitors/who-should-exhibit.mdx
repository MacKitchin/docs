Connect marketplaces work for hospitality suppliers who sell into group and meetings business - not general consumer travel or retail. If your buyer is a meeting planner sourcing rooms, venues, or services for an event, you are the audience these marketplaces are built for.

## Supplier categories that fit

Connect's supplier universe spans roughly **13,800 accounts across 29 supplier categories** in Salesforce, identified by `Primary_Category__c LIKE 'Supplier-%'`. The categories map onto a handful of practical groups:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Destinations" icon="map-pin">
    Convention and visitors bureaus (CVBs) and destination marketing organisations (DMOs) promoting a city or region to meeting planners.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Hotels, resorts, and venues" icon="building">
    Hotels, resorts, convention centres, and unique venues with meeting space and room blocks to sell.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Event technology and production" icon="tv">
    AV and production companies, registration and matchmaking platforms, apps, and other event-tech vendors.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Travel and transportation" icon="plane">
    Airlines and ground transportation providers serving group travel to meetings and events.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Experiential and gifting" icon="gift">
    Experiential agencies, gifting and merchandise companies, and other suppliers of onsite attendee experiences.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The fit test

Ask three questions before buying a package:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Do you sell to meeting planners, not consumers?">
    Connect's hosted buyers are event, meeting, and travel planners sourcing on behalf of an organisation - not individual travellers. If your product is consumer-facing, a Connect marketplace is the wrong channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Can you fulfil group business at scale?">
    Hosted buyers qualify by sourcing 50 or more rooms on peak nights, and some tracks require materially more (see [Who qualifies](/hosted-buyers/who-qualifies)). If you cannot accommodate group room blocks, meeting space, or group rates, the room is not shopping for what you sell.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Does a track match your market?">
    Corporate, Association, Sports, Specialty, International, Citywide, Business Travel, Diversity, and Faith buyers all attend in different mixes at different events. Match your ideal customer profile to the track breakdown on the [event pages](/events/overview) before choosing which marketplace to attend.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you answered yes to all three, a Connect marketplace should outperform a traditional tradeshow for you. The appointment model means every meeting is with someone who already has budget, authority, and a live sourcing need - see [Business model](/company/business-model) for how the matching works.
</Tip>

## What doesn't fit

- Consumer travel or retail brands with no group-sourcing product
- Vendors without the capacity to fulfil group room blocks or meeting space at the volumes hosted buyers source
- Businesses looking for a passive trade-show floor rather than a scheduled-appointment model - Connect's whole design assumes you want structured meetings, not walk-up traffic

If you are unsure which category or track fits, see [Package options](/exhibitors/packages) for pricing by tier, or contact the Connect sales team.
