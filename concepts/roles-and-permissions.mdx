ConnectMeetings uses a role-based permission model to ensure every user can do exactly what they need to — and nothing more. Roles are assigned at the organization level and apply across all meetings in your account. There are four roles: **Admin**, **Organizer**, **Participant**, and **View Only**. Each role is designed for a specific type of user in a public-sector meeting workflow.

## Roles Overview

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Admin">
    Admins have full access to everything in the ConnectMeetings organization. This role is intended for IT administrators and senior staff responsible for the platform.

    **Admins can:**
    - Manage organization settings, including branding and the public portal
    - Add, remove, and reassign roles for all users
    - Access and manage all meetings, regardless of whether they were invited
    - Approve or unlock agendas and minutes for any meeting
    - Manage billing, subscription, and integrations (including Microsoft 365)
    - View the full audit trail for any meeting or record
    - Export any data from the platform
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Organizer">
    Organizers are the staff members who plan and run meetings — typically clerks, executive assistants, committee coordinators, or department managers.

    **Organizers can:**
    - Create new meetings and configure all meeting details
    - Build, edit, and submit agendas for approval
    - Upload and manage agenda attachments
    - Invite and manage attendees for meetings they own
    - Open the live minutes editor and record notes, motions, and votes
    - Create and assign action items during and after a meeting
    - Submit minutes for approval and distribute approved copies
    - Export minutes as PDF and action items as CSV
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Participant">
    Participants are the people who attend meetings — elected members, board directors, committee members, or invited staff.

    **Participants can:**
    - View published agendas and attached documents for meetings they are invited to
    - Access the meeting link and any virtual conferencing details
    - View approved minutes for meetings they attended
    - Receive action items assigned to them and update the status of their own action items
    - Add progress notes to their own action items
    - View (but not edit) action items assigned to others in the same meeting
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="View Only">
    View Only users have read-only access to published meeting materials. This role suits members of the public, auditors, or internal stakeholders who need visibility without the ability to participate or edit.

    **View Only users can:**
    - View published agendas for meetings they have been granted access to
    - View approved minutes once they are published
    - Download PDF exports of approved minutes (if permitted by the organizer)

    **View Only users cannot:**
    - Attend meetings or access virtual meeting links
    - View Draft or In Progress content of any kind
    - Receive, create, or update action items
    - Make any changes to any record
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Assigning Roles

Only Admins can assign or change roles. To update a user's role:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Members">
    Go to **Admin → Members** in the left navigation panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the user">
    Search for the user by name or email address in the Members list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the role">
    Select the user's current role in the **Role** column. A dropdown appears with the four available roles. Select the new role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The change takes effect immediately. The user's permissions update on their next page load — no sign-out required. ConnectMeetings logs the role change in the organization audit trail.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Organizations on the **Enterprise plan** have access to custom Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Enterprise RBAC lets you define granular permission sets — for example, allowing a specific Organizer to manage only certain committees, or granting a Participant the ability to view Draft agendas. Contact your ConnectMeetings account manager or visit **Admin → Roles → Custom Roles** to learn more.
</Note>

<Warning>
  On the **Starter plan**, each organization can have only one Admin. If you need to reassign the Admin role to a different user, the current Admin must first contact ConnectMeetings Support to initiate the transfer. Upgrading to the **Professional or Enterprise plan** removes this restriction and allows multiple Admins.
</Warning>
