An agenda is the structured plan for a meeting. In ConnectMeetings, agendas are first-class objects — not just documents attached to a meeting, but interactive records that drive the flow of the session, populate the minutes editor, and become part of the permanent governance record. Organizers build and refine the agenda before the meeting; participants receive it automatically when it is approved and distributed.

## Agenda Structure

Every agenda has two layers: a **header** and a list of **agenda items**.

The **header** captures meeting-level information that appears at the top of the printed and distributed agenda.

- Meeting title and type
- Date, time, and location
- Chair and secretary names
- Any preamble or standing notices required by your governing procedures

**Agenda items** form the numbered body of the agenda. Each item contains the following fields.

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Item number | Auto-assigned; drag to reorder and numbers update automatically |
| Title | A short, clear description of the matter |
| Presenter | The person responsible for introducing the item |
| Time allocation | Estimated minutes allotted to the item |
| Item type | The category of business (see below) |
| Attachments | Supporting documents uploaded to the item |
| Notes / background | Internal context or briefing notes visible to participants |

## Agenda Item Types

Classifying each item helps participants prepare and helps the minutes editor pre-format the record correctly.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Information Item" icon="circle-info">
    A report or update provided for awareness only. No vote or decision is required. The item is recorded in the minutes as received.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Action Item" icon="check-circle">
    A matter requiring a formal decision, motion, or resolution. The minutes editor will prompt for a motion, mover, seconder, and vote outcome.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Presentation" icon="presentation-screen">
    A structured presentation by a staff member, external consultant, or delegation. Presentations may be followed by a question period.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Consent Item" icon="list-check">
    A routine item grouped with others for a single omnibus motion, typically approving standard reports or minutes. Any member can pull a consent item for separate discussion.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Public Comment" icon="comments">
    A designated period for registered members of the public to address the body. ConnectMeetings tracks registered speakers and time limits.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Agenda Approval Workflow

Before an agenda is distributed to participants, it moves through an approval workflow to ensure accuracy and compliance.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draft">
    The organizer builds the agenda, adds items, sets time allocations, and uploads attachments. The agenda is only visible to organizers and admins at this stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submitted for Approval">
    The organizer submits the agenda to the designated approver — typically the meeting chair or a senior administrator. Approvers receive an email notification with a review link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reviewed">
    The approver opens the agenda, makes or requests changes, and either approves it or sends it back to Draft with comments. Changes at this stage are tracked so the organizer can see exactly what was amended.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approved">
    The agenda is locked and marked as the official version. Participants are notified and the agenda is distributed automatically. No further edits can be made without unlocking (see note below).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Once an agenda is approved, it is locked to protect the integrity of the official record. If a last-minute change is required — such as adding an emergency item — an Admin or the meeting chair can unlock the agenda, make the amendment, and re-approve it. Every unlock and re-approval event is logged in the meeting's audit trail.
</Note>

## Attachments

You can attach supporting documents directly to individual agenda items. Attachments are distributed to participants alongside the agenda and are stored in the meeting record permanently.

Supported file types include PDF, Word (`.docx`), Excel (`.xlsx`), and PowerPoint (`.pptx`). Each attachment can be marked as **public** (included in any public-facing agenda publication) or **confidential** (visible only to authenticated participants with appropriate permissions).

To add an attachment, open any agenda item in the agenda builder and select **Add Attachment**. Files are scanned for viruses on upload and stored securely in your organization's ConnectMeetings file library.

## Distribution

When an agenda is approved, ConnectMeetings distributes it to all meeting participants automatically.

- **Email** — Each participant receives an email containing the agenda as a PDF attachment, plus a link to the interactive agenda in ConnectMeetings.
- **Calendar** — The meeting's calendar invitation (sent when the meeting was published) is updated to include the agenda PDF as an attachment, so it appears directly in the participant's Microsoft 365 calendar event.
- **Public portal** — If your organization has enabled the public portal, the approved agenda is published there immediately upon approval, meeting any statutory notice requirements you have configured.

You can also trigger manual redistribution at any time from **Meetings → [Meeting Name] → Agenda → Redistribute**.

<Tip>
  Use agenda templates to save time on recurring meetings. Templates let you pre-populate standard items — such as Call to Order, Approval of Previous Minutes, and Adjournment — so organizers only need to add the session-specific items each time. Manage your templates at [Agendas → Templates](/agendas/templates).
</Tip>
