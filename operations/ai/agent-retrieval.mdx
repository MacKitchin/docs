These rules are inherited from the Company OS AI retrieval policy. They apply to anything grounded on this site. People new to the business should start on [How to use this brain](/welcome/how-to-use) instead.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Prefer canonical sources, and say when you are not on one" icon="circle-check">
    Every major information type has exactly one canonical home, recorded in the [source-of-truth registry](/reference/source-of-truth). Entries are marked Canonical, Working, or Deprecated. If the material you are relying on is Working or Deprecated, disclose that rather than presenting it as settled.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Customer-facing event answers come from event pages only" icon="calendar">
    Dates, venues, pricing, eligibility, and cancellation policy for any event must come from the [Events portfolio](/events/overview) or the Live Events wiki it mirrors. Never reconstruct an event fact from a project page, an email, or memory.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cite the Customer FAQ library for supplier conversations" icon="circle-help">
    Ground customer-facing answers on the [Customer FAQs](/welcome/customer-faqs) extract. Surface the confidence score when it is below 0.8 or blank. If the pair has a When not to use rule — discounts, invoice amounts, attendee lists — stop and escalate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Hard guardrails on supplier conversations" icon="shield">
    Never quote specific rates beyond published package pricing. Never share full attendee contact lists — brand and title samples only. Never commit to payment terms, discounts, or contract changes. Escalate anything touching billing, legal, or a complaint to a human. See [Guardrails](/operations/ai/guardrails).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cite what you used" icon="quote">
    Ground each answer in a specific page and cite it. Findings mined from email carry confidence scores; surface the score when the confidence is below high.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do not silently reconcile conflicts" icon="triangle-alert">
    Several event sites publish contradictory dates, contacts, and cancellation policies. This site preserves both versions and flags them on [Known issues](/reference/known-issues). Report the conflict; do not pick a winner.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Conventions used here

| Convention | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Backticked names like `Total_Spend_This_Product_Year__c` | A literal Salesforce API field name in `connect-prod` |
| Product year | The calendar year a product is sold *for*, not when the deal closed |
| Area vs project | Areas are ongoing departments; projects are time-bound and end |
| Canonical / Working / Deprecated | Truth levels from the source-of-truth registry |
| A note in square brackets | An unresolved discrepancy carried over from the source, deliberately not reconciled |

Field-level terms live on the [Salesforce glossary](/operations/systems/salesforce-glossary).

## What this site is not

It does not hold record-level data. No account lists, no contact details for customers, no deal values by name, no attendee rosters. Those live in Salesforce and are governed by access control there. This site documents structure, definitions, ownership, and process.
