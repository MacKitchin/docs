## Integration Architecture

Connect Meetings operates across multiple interconnected systems:

### Core Systems
- **Salesforce**: Customer data, opportunities, orders, and reporting
- **Visit Create**: Event registration, badge printing, and on-site operations
- **Shopify/Stripe**: Commerce and payment processing
- **Notion**: Company OS and documentation
