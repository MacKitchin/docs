## Overview

An institutional-knowledge base mined from historical Salesforce correspondence with **supplier accounts** - exhibitors, sponsors, advertisers, DMOs and CVBs, venues, AV, production, and technology vendors. It turns tribal knowledge that lived only in reps' inboxes into a searchable, retrieval-ready knowledge base for both employees and Agentforce agents.

The next phase scales the pipeline from the v1 sample to the full 2025-2026 corpus of roughly **26,600 logged supplier emails**, to produce a true ranked top-100 question list and quantified deal correlations.

## Scope and method

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **Supplier cohort** | Accounts where `Primary_Category__c LIKE 'Supplier-%'` - roughly 13,800 accounts across 29 supplier categories |
| **Why that filter** | `Account_Sub_Type__c = 'Supplier'` returns only 30 records; category was the far better signal |
| **Activity profiled** | 80,612 `EmailMessage` records (67k outbound, 13k inbound) and 416,578 `Task` records, of which 385k are logged emails |
| **v1 sample** | The most recent ~200 days of supplier email threads, to validate the pipeline end to end and seed each knowledge base with grounded findings |
| **Confidence** | Every finding carries a confidence score and an occurrence signal; minimum corroboration for anything treated as policy is two independent sources |

## Governance

<Warning>
  This is the most sensitive project in the portfolio, and its governance rules are non-negotiable.

  - Raw email bodies are **never** stored. Only generalised, PII-redacted knowledge.
  - Pricing, legal, contract, payment, complaint, and PII content is flagged High or Critical sensitivity and routed to the **Knowledge Candidates** review queue rather than auto-published. Six sensitive items are currently held there awaiting human review.
  - Single-source items are never treated as policy.
  - Source Salesforce record IDs are retained on every finding for auditability.
</Warning>

## Scaling to the full corpus

1. Iterate the supplier cohort across the full 2025-2026 history in windowed batches, deduplicating by thread.
2. Join activity to opportunity stage and outcome to quantify which questions and objections correlate with won deals.
3. Add numeric Frequency and Rank properties to Customer FAQs and build a sorted top-100 view.
4. Re-run scoring, promote review-queue items after human approval, update Last Observed.
