No completed project is currently listed in the Company Brain navigation. Master Projects in Company OS remains the canonical register.

## Archive rules

- Move a project here only after its canonical Master Projects row has a terminal status.
- Preserve the final outcome, owner, dates, decisions, unresolved follow-up, and replacement link.
- Do not delete a superseded project page when it explains a material decision or data lineage.
- Mark any continuing operational area separately; an area does not become a completed project.

<Info>
  Active work remains on [Active projects](/operations/projects/overview). Deprecated databases and knowledge sources remain recorded in the [Source-of-truth registry](/reference/source-of-truth).
</Info>
