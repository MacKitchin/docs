Company OS is the internal Notion workspace that organises governed company knowledge using a shared master-database model and PARA-style navigation.

## What it owns

| Information | Canonical Notion surface |
| --- | --- |
| People and team records | Master People, with Team HQ as the front door |
| Projects and initiatives | Master Projects |
| Tasks | Master Tasks |
| Event facts | Live Events wiki |
| SOPs and internal documents | Master Docs and relevant knowledge hubs |
| Source status and ownership | Source-of-truth registry |
| AI agents, prompts, and tools | AI & Automation Hub and AI & Agent Library |

## Relationship to this site

The Company Brain is a retrieval-friendly mirror. It may reorganise and explain Company OS material, but it does not supersede the owned Notion record.
