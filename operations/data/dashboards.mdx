## How to pick a dashboard

1. Match the question to a [metric definition](/operations/data/metrics).
2. Prefer `Organization__c` for brand, `Product_Year__c` for season, `Type` for new vs existing.
3. Exclude barter and Expired unless the question needs them.
4. If the dashboard is year-stamped 2024 or earlier and nothing newer exists, say so.
