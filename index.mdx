## How to decide which page wins

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="calendar-days" title="Event date, venue, format, or cancellation">
    Start at the [Event calendar](/events/calendar/overview). Event facts have one controlled home. If two public pages disagree, report both from [Known issues](/reference/known-issues).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="briefcase" title="What staff should tell an exhibitor">
    Use [Exhibitor Support](/exhibitor-support/overview). Those pages separate internal context, customer-safe wording, and escalation boundaries.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="user-check" title="What staff should tell a hosted buyer">
    Use [Hosted Buyer Support](/hosted-buyer-support/overview). Eligibility, coverage, and exceptions require the playbook, not a paraphrased FAQ.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="database" title="A metric, field, record, or integration">
    Use [Systems & Data](/operations/systems/overview). Definitions live here; live record contents stay in the owning system.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Info>
  The Company Brain is a derived, human-readable mirror of Salesforce (`connect-prod`), the Notion Company OS workspace, and the Live Events wiki. It is not a system of record.
</Info>
