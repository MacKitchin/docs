## The business in one paragraph

We run marketplace-style appointment events for the MICE industry (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, Exhibitions). Hospitality suppliers - convention and visitors bureaus, destination marketing organisations, hotels, venues, AV and event-tech vendors - buy packages to meet pre-qualified event planners in scheduled one-to-one appointments. The planners, called **hosted buyers**, attend on a sponsored basis: we cover flights and hotel in exchange for a committed appointment schedule. Alongside the events we sell media and advertising through BizBash, TSNN, and Connect Travel. Revenue therefore has two engines: event packages and media insertion orders.
