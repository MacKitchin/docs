</Warning>

## How to decide which page wins

| If the question is about... | Start here | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Event date, venue, format, eligibility, appointment count, or cancellation | [Event calendar](/events/calendar/overview) | Event facts have one controlled home and retain source dates and conflicts. |
| What staff should tell an exhibitor | [Exhibitor Support](/exhibitor-support/overview) | The pages separate internal context, customer-safe wording, and escalation boundaries. |
| What staff should tell a hosted buyer | [Hosted Buyer Support](/hosted-buyer-support/overview) | Eligibility, coverage, commitments, and exceptions require role-specific handling. |
| A person, team, or approval | [Who owns what](/people/who-owns-what) | Ownership and known gaps are explicit. |
| A metric, field, record, or integration | [Systems & Data](/operations/systems/overview) | Definitions are separated from live record contents. |
| Conflicting or weak information | [Source of truth](/reference/source-of-truth) and [Known issues](/reference/known-issues) | Canonical, Working, and Deprecated sources are treated differently. |

<Info>
  The Company Brain is a derived, human-readable mirror of Salesforce (`connect-prod`), the Notion Company OS workspace, and the Live Events wiki. It is not a system of record. When this site and an owning system disagree, report the difference and follow the source and conflict rules rather than silently choosing a version.
</Info>

## The business in one paragraph

Connect Meetings & Events runs marketplace-style appointment events for the MICE industry — meetings, incentives, conferences, and exhibitions. Hospitality suppliers buy packages to meet qualified event planners in scheduled one-to-one appointments. Hosted buyers attend on a sponsored basis in exchange for programme commitments. The business also sells media and advertising through BizBash, TSNN, and Connect Travel. Read [Company & Strategy](/company/overview) for the structure and [How the business works](/company/business-model) for the commercial model.
