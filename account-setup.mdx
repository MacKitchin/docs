Once you have created your ConnectMeetings account, a few minutes of configuration will make the platform work exactly the way your organization needs it. This guide covers creating your organization profile, inviting team members, configuring settings, and managing your plan.

## Creating your organization

When you sign up, ConnectMeetings automatically creates an organization tied to your account. You can update your organization's profile at any time from **Settings > Organization**.

- **Organization name** — enter the full legal name of your organization as you want it to appear on published agendas, minutes documents, and email headers.
- **Sector** — select the category that best describes your organization. ConnectMeetings uses this to suggest relevant meeting templates and governance workflows. Available sectors include:
  - Municipal government
  - Regional district
  - School board
  - Healthcare authority
  - Crown corporation
  - Other public sector
- **Primary contact** — set an administrator email address for account-level notifications such as billing receipts and security alerts.

Click **Save** to apply your changes. Your organization name will appear immediately on all newly generated documents.

## Inviting team members

<Warning>
  Only users with the **Admin** role can invite new members to your organization or make changes to billing. If you need to invite someone and do not see the **Invite** button, ask your organization's Admin to grant you the Admin role first.
</Warning>

To add a team member, go to **Admin > Members** and click **Invite Member**. Enter the person's email address and select a role, then click **Send Invitation**. They will receive an email with a link to join your organization.

ConnectMeetings offers four roles:

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Full access to all settings, billing, member management, and all meetings. |
| **Organizer** | Create and manage meetings, build agendas, record minutes, and assign action items. |
| **Participant** | Attend meetings, view agendas, and complete assigned action items. |
| **View Only** | Read-only access to published agendas and approved minutes documents. |

Assign the most restrictive role that still meets the person's needs. You can change a member's role at any time from the **Members** list.

## Organization settings

Fine-tune how ConnectMeetings behaves for your entire organization under **Settings > Organization**.

**Branding**

<Note>
  Custom branding — including your logo and accent colors on published agendas and minutes — is available on the **Professional** plan and above. On the Starter plan, ConnectMeetings branding appears on all generated documents.
</Note>

- **Logo** — upload a PNG or SVG file (recommended size: 200 × 60 px). Your logo appears on agenda headers, minutes documents, and the member invitation email.
- **Accent color** — choose a hex color to match your organization's visual identity. This color is used for headings and buttons in generated documents.

**Time and scheduling defaults**

- **Timezone** — set your organization's default timezone. All meeting times are displayed in this timezone unless an individual member has set a personal timezone in their profile.
- **Default meeting duration** — choose a default length (30, 60, or 90 minutes) that pre-populates the duration field when any Organizer creates a new meeting.

**Notification defaults**

- **Agenda published** — notify all attendees when an agenda is published. Enabled by default.
- **Reminder before meeting** — send a reminder email and push notification to attendees. You can set the lead time to 15 minutes, 1 hour, 24 hours, or a custom interval.
- **Action item due** — notify action item owners 24 hours before their task is due. Enabled by default.

Individual members can override these defaults from their own **Profile > Notifications** settings.

## Plan & billing

To view your current plan and usage, go to **Settings > Billing**.

ConnectMeetings offers three plans:

- **Starter** — up to 10 members and 20 meetings per month. Includes core scheduling, agendas, and minutes. Best for small committees or pilot deployments.
- **Professional** — unlimited members and meetings, custom branding, advanced analytics, and Microsoft 365 integration. Best for mid-size councils or boards.
- **Enterprise** — everything in Professional plus SSO, dedicated support, custom data retention policies, and a signed BAA for regulated industries. Contact sales for pricing.

To upgrade, click **Upgrade Plan** on the Billing page and follow the checkout steps. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the remainder of your billing cycle.

To switch from monthly to annual billing — or to discuss an Enterprise contract — contact the ConnectMeetings team at [sales@connectmeetings.ca](mailto:sales@connectmeetings.ca).
