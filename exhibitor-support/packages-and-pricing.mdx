## What's included in every supplier package

- Access to all appointment blocks matching the supplier's tier
- Opening reception and hosted networking events
- Education sessions (3\+ hours at regionals; 14 to 15 hours at flagships)
- Marketplace listing in the attendee directory and event app
- Onsite badge and access to hosted meals as scheduled

Not included: the supplier's own travel, hotel room outside the supplier block, and any event amplification add-ons.

Hosted meals, afternoon amp-ups, the hosted buyer amenity shop, closing-night parties, and other sponsorship opportunities are sold through a per-event prospectus. Pricing is not published; request the current prospectus from the sponsorship contact for the event the supplier is targeting.

## Booking and cancellation

<Info>
  Standard cancellation: full refund minus a 10% administration charge if cancelled **57 days or more** before the event. No refund inside 56 days. Terms vary by event; always confirm against the Terms & Conditions on the specific event site.
</Info>

Supplier hotel room blocks typically close **3 to 4 weeks** before the event. A supplier that misses the block may have to book independently at the available rate.
