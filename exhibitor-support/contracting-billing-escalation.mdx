Use this page to route sensitive commercial questions. It does not replace the agreement, invoice, Salesforce record, or responsible human owner.

## Non-negotiable boundary

<Warning>
  Staff and agents must not invent, change, or commit payment terms, discounts, contract language, cancellation outcomes, unpublished prices, or expired offers.
</Warning>

## Route the request

| Request | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Published package price | Confirm the source date on [Packages and pricing](/exhibitor-support/packages-and-pricing), then verify current availability with the sales owner |
| Specific discount or expired promotion | Escalate to the account's sales owner |
| Agreement or contractual language | Escalate to Legal routing and the sales owner |
| Invoice amount, payment status, or payment schedule | Escalate to human Finance routing; Finance is currently an unowned area |
| Cancellation or transfer | Confirm the registrant type and event-specific source, then escalate any exception |
| Complaint | Escalate to the named account owner |

## Evidence staff should capture

- Event and product requested
- Account and opportunity identifiers in Salesforce, without copying record data into this site
- Published source and capture date for any quoted package
- The exact question or requested exception
- The sales or service owner receiving the escalation
