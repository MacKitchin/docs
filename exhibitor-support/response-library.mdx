Use this page to choose the right source before replying to an exhibitor or supplier.

## Response routes

| Question | Start with | Then check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is this event a fit? | [Fit and qualification](/exhibitor-support/fit-and-qualification) | Event portfolio and sales owner |
| What packages or prices are published? | [Packages and pricing](/exhibitor-support/packages-and-pricing) | Source date and current availability |
| How are appointments built? | [Appointment model](/exhibitor-support/appointment-model) | Event-specific platform and deadlines |
| What happens before or onsite? | [Event preparation](/exhibitor-support/preparing-for-the-event) and [Onsite experience](/exhibitor-support/onsite-experience) | Live event page |
| Is there a standard answer? | [Customer FAQs](/welcome/customer-faqs) | Confidence and When-not-to-use rule |
| Who decides an exception? | [Who owns what](/people/who-owns-what) | Guardrails and named account owner |

## Customer-safe versus internal-only

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Customer-safe" icon="circle-check">
    Published event facts, published package contents, the standard appointment model, preparation guidance, and approved FAQ language.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Internal-only" icon="lock">
    Salesforce IDs and defects, webhook details, ownership gaps, unpublished rates, internal confidence scoring, private source locations, and unresolved analysis.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Warning>
  Billing, invoices, legal, contract changes, complaints, specific discounts, named attendee lists, and unpublished sponsorship rates always require escalation.
</Warning>
