Use this page to make an initial fit assessment. It is not approval to quote a package, promise appointments, or confirm participation.

<Info>
  **Audience:** Connect staff and approved internal agents. Confirm the specific event, package, availability, and current terms with the responsible sales owner before making a commitment.
</Info>

## Strong-fit supplier categories

- Convention and visitors bureaus and destination marketing organisations
- Hotels, resorts, convention centres, and venues
- Audiovisual, production, and event-technology providers
- Airlines, ground-transportation providers, gifting companies, and experiential partners
- Other suppliers with a credible product for qualified meeting and event planners

## Fit questions for staff

| Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Does the supplier sell into meetings and events? | The appointment audience must be commercially relevant. |
| Can the supplier serve the geography or buyer track attached to the event? | Regional and specialty portfolios have different audiences. |
| Is the supplier seeking qualified appointments rather than general floor traffic? | The Connect value proposition is scheduled access. |
| Does the supplier have an offer and onsite representative able to make decisions? | Short appointments work only when the supplier can act. |
| Is the requested product currently published and available? | Package and sponsorship availability is source-dated, not live inventory. |
