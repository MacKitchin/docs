## Staff guidance

Use this split before sending a reply. When in doubt, keep it internal and escalate.

## Customer-safe information

- Published event facts from the [Event calendar](/events/calendar/overview) after checking Known issues
- Published package contents and prices from [Packages and published pricing](/exhibitor-support/packages-and-pricing), with source date
- The standard [appointment model](/exhibitor-support/appointment-model)
- Preparation and onsite guidance on the playbook pages
- Approved FAQ short answers

## Internal-only context

- Salesforce IDs, defects, and webhook details
- Ownership gaps
- Unpublished rates and sponsorship prospectus pricing
- Internal confidence scoring
- Private source locations and unresolved analysis

## Limits and escalation

Customer-safe wording on a page does not make the full page suitable to forward. See [Exceptions and escalation](/exhibitor-support/exceptions-and-escalation).
