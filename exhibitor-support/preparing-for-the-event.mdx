Use this checklist to help a supplier prepare for the event and protect the value of its appointment package.

## Key deadlines to track

| Deadline | Typical timing | What happens if the supplier misses it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Complete company profile in the event platform | Weeks before requests open | The supplier may not appear correctly in buyer searches |
| Submit appointment requests | Before the published request cutoff | The supplier matches on residuals, not preferences |
| Book supplier hotel block | 3 to 4 weeks before the event | The supplier pays the applicable independent rate |
| Standard cancellation cutoff | 57 days before | Inside 56 days, no refund |
| Confirm onsite team roster | Ahead of badge production | Delayed or reissued badges onsite |

Exact dates vary by event. Confirm against the specific event site: [Flagship marketplaces](/events/connect-marketplace) or [Regional marketplaces](/events/regional).

<Info>
  Recent supplier hotel block deadlines for reference: Connect Marketplace 3 August, Connect Texas 5 October, Connect West 14 October, Connect South 16 October, Winter Marketplace 27 October.
</Info>

## Team readiness

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who to send">
    Advise the supplier to send people with **decision authority** on the offer being presented: rate cards, dates, complimentary offerings, and creative concessions. Appointments are short; buyers quickly evaluate whether the supplier can act.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many people">
    Match the supplier's team size to the supplier's appointment volume. One rep can realistically run a 40-appointment schedule at a flagship over three days, but they will be depleted by day three. Two reps splitting the schedule is more sustainable and gives the supplier a walk-up presence.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Brief the team">
    Everyone should know: the buyer track breakdown, the supplier's top-priority requests, the supplier's standard offer, the supplier's walk-away terms, and where the concierge desk is for schedule changes.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Collateral to bring

- **One-page leave-behind** with rates, capacities, and dates. Not a full brochure.
- **Digital pack** delivered by email or QR code during the appointment. Buyers rarely carry paper home.
- **Case studies** relevant to the buyer track the supplier is meeting (corporate, association, sports, etc.).
- **Business cards or digital equivalent** for the walk-up moments outside appointments.

## Logistics

- **Book flights** with schedule flexibility on the closing day; last appointment blocks run into the afternoon
- **Book the supplier hotel block** before it closes; do not book outside for a small saving and then miss the block
- **Confirm shipping** for booth materials, giveaways, or samples with the venue and Connect operations team
- **Check code of conduct** and any dress guidance published for the specific event

<Tip>
  If the supplier is attending multiple events in a season, route an integrated media and sponsorship discussion to Connect sales rather than treating each event as an isolated purchase.
</Tip>
