## Staff guidance

Escalate rather than invent. Capture event, account identifiers in Salesforce, the published source and date, the exact ask, and the owner receiving the handoff. Do not copy record-level data into this site.

| Exception | Goes to |
| --- | --- |
| Specific discount or expired promotion | Account sales owner |
| Agreement or contractual language | Legal routing and the sales owner |
| Invoice amount, payment status, or schedule | Stephen Han (Finance) |
| Complaint | Named account owner |
| Unpublished rate | Do not quote. Sales owner if a published alternative exists |

## Customer-safe information

That the request has been routed, and any already-published package or event fact you have verified.

## Internal-only context

Why the exception exists, Salesforce identifiers, and internal owner gaps.

## Limits and escalation

Staff and agents must not invent, change, or commit payment terms, discounts, contract language, cancellation outcomes, unpublished prices, or expired offers.
