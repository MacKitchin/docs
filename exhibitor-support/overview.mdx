Use this section when advising or supporting a hospitality supplier before, during, or after a Connect marketplace. It explains the customer journey while preserving the internal verification and escalation boundaries staff need.

<Info>
  **Audience:** Connect staff and approved internal agents. This is not a public exhibitor guide. Staff may adapt customer-safe facts in conversation, but must not share internal system details, source conflicts, unpublished rates, or ownership gaps.
</Info>

## Suppliers this section covers

Hospitality suppliers exhibiting at Connect marketplace events, including:

- Convention and visitors bureaus (CVBs) and destination marketing organisations (DMOs)
- Hotels, resorts, and convention centres
- Audiovisual, production, and event technology vendors
- Airlines, ground transportation, and gifting or experiential partners

For staff supporting event planners attending on a sponsored basis, use [Hosted Buyer Support](/hosted-buyer-support/overview).

## How a Connect marketplace works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Qualified buyers are recruited">
    Event planners apply to the hosted buyer programme and are vetted for scale, sourcing authority, and event history. See [Business model](/company/business-model) for the full qualification bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Suppliers buy appointment packages">
    Suppliers purchase packages priced by the number of one-to-one appointments. Regional events start at \$4,250 for 12 appointments; flagships offer higher tiers with more appointments and preferred placement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Appointments are matched in advance">
    Requests are collected from both sides and matched, often with automation support, before anyone travels. The supplier arrives with a schedule rather than relying on floor traffic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Suppliers meet buyers on the marketplace floor">
    Appointment blocks run in fixed windows across the event days, alongside education, networking, and hosted meals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Staff support onsite rebooking">
    A dedicated rebooking lounge at the flagship lets returning suppliers commit to the next event before leaving. Renewals are the core of the business.
  </Step>
</Steps>
