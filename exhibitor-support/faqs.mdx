## Staff guidance

Use the [Customer FAQs](/welcome/customer-faqs) extract. Prefer the **Short answer**. Surface confidence below 0.8. Stop when **When not to use** applies. Then confirm event facts on the [Event calendar](/events/calendar/overview).

Supplier, media, and contracting pairs:

- [Supplier packages, appointments, and events](/welcome/customer-faqs#supplier-packages-appointments-and-events)
- [Media, creative, and campaigns](/welcome/customer-faqs#media-creative-and-campaigns)
- [Contracting, payment, and discounts](/welcome/customer-faqs#contracting-payment-and-discounts)

## Customer-safe information

The Short answer field, quoted rather than paraphrased into a new policy.

## Internal-only context

Frequency, rank, confidence scores, When-not-to-use annotations, and Knowledge Candidates items that are not on the extract.

## Limits and escalation

Do not invent a thirtieth pair. Billing, discounts, invoice timing, and attendee lists require a human. See [Exceptions and escalation](/exhibitor-support/exceptions-and-escalation).
