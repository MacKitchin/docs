The appointment schedule is the core value of a Connect marketplace package.

## Timeline

<Steps>
  <Step title="Package purchased">
    The supplier's appointment count is locked in based on the supplier's tier. Higher tiers (Deal Closer at the flagship) also lock in a **top-five requests guaranteed** promise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Directory and profile go live">
    The supplier's company is listed in the attendee directory and event app. Hosted buyers can browse suppliers and start marking requests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Requests window opens">
    Both sides submit requests: suppliers pick the buyers they want to see, hosted buyers pick the suppliers they want to see.

  <Step title="Matching runs">
    Requests are reconciled, often AI-assisted, into a mutual-interest appointment schedule. Preferred tiers get priority on their top requests.

  <Step title="Schedules published">
    The supplier receives a personalised appointment schedule ahead of the event, showing each appointment time, location, and buyer.

    A concierge desk handles substitutions, no-shows, and last-minute swaps.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How matching works

Matching combines three inputs:

1. **Explicit requests** from suppliers and hosted buyers
2. **Profile fit** based on buyer track, geography, budget size, and supplier category
3. **Tier priority** so Deal Closer packages resolve first, then Elite Connecter, and so on

Deal Closer suppliers at the flagship are guaranteed their top five requests. Lower tiers are matched on best-effort mutual interest.

<Info>
  Hosted buyers commit to a full appointment schedule as a condition of their sponsored attendance. The \$200 refundable deposit is returned only when they complete the assigned appointments, which is what keeps the schedule live. See [Hosted buyer deposit](/hosted-buyer-support/application-and-approval) for the mechanism.
</Info>

## Appointment mechanics

- Appointments are one-to-one between one supplier representative and one hosted buyer
- Meetings are short, focused conversations (some events describe them as **nine minutes**; confirm the exact duration for the supplier's event before quoting it internally)
- Blocks run in fixed windows across the event days, for example the flagship runs **10:00-12:00 and 15:00-17:00** on days two and three
- Missed appointments count as no-shows against the buyer deposit

<Warning>
  Appointment duration and count numbers vary between event pages and marketing materials. Do not promise a specific meeting length to internal stakeholders without checking the confirmation packet for that specific event.
</Warning>

## Getting the most from the supplier's requests

- Submit requests early, before the deadline. Late requests match against whatever is left.
- Prioritise **quality over quantity**: five buyers the supplier can genuinely close beats twenty long shots.
- Use the profile fields (buyer track, geography, event size) to filter, not just company names.
- If the supplier has a Power Pass representative in addition to a scheduled representative, position that person for walk-up conversations rather than scheduled appointment slots.
