This page connects the commercial close to event readiness. Exact forms, cutoffs, and platform steps vary by event and remain owned by the live event process.

## Standard staff path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the deal and product">
    Verify the Opportunity and line items in `connect-prod`. Do not use this site to infer a named customer's current status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect attendee details through the approved route">
    The Customer FAQ library records a post-signature attendee-details step, often using DocuSign. Use the current event workflow rather than sending an unverified form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or verify the event-platform profile">
    Confirm that the supplier organisation, attendees, package, and appointment entitlement appear in the applicable platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track event-specific deadlines">
    Profile completion, request submission, hotel blocks, creative delivery, badge production, and cancellation dates come from the event source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm appointment readiness">
    Route the supplier to [Appointment model](/exhibitor-support/appointment-model) and [Preparing for the event](/exhibitor-support/preparing-for-the-event).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The current source set does not establish one universal supplier-registration workflow for every event. Do not fabricate a URL, platform, deadline, or automation path.
</Warning>
