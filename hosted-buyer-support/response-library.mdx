Use this page to choose the right source before replying to a hosted buyer or applicant.

## Response routes

| Question | Start with | Then check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does the planner qualify? | [Eligibility](/hosted-buyer-support/eligibility) | Event track and Hosted Buyer Experience team |
| What is covered? | [Coverage](/hosted-buyer-support/coverage) | Event-specific booking rules |
| What must the buyer complete? | [Commitments](/hosted-buyer-support/commitments) | Application and applicable cancellation policy |
| Where is an application or registration? | [Application and approval](/hosted-buyer-support/application-and-approval) | Current event source; do not invent a URL |
| What happens before or onsite? | [Preparation](/hosted-buyer-support/preparing-for-the-event) and [Onsite experience](/hosted-buyer-support/onsite-experience) | Live agenda and assigned HBX contact |
| Is there a standard answer? | [Customer FAQs](/welcome/customer-faqs) | Confidence and When-not-to-use rule |

## Customer-safe versus internal-only

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Customer-safe" icon="circle-check">
    Published eligibility, coverage, event facts, appointment expectations, approved application guidance, and approved FAQ language.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Internal-only" icon="lock">
    Salesforce and Visit IDs, webhook routing, sync defects, internal confidence scores, private source locations, and unresolved ownership gaps.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Warning>
  Deposit disputes, cancellation exceptions, billing, complaints, named attendee data, legal terms, and unverified travel promises require human escalation.
</Warning>
