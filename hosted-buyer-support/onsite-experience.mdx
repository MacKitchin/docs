Use this page to set expectations and route common onsite questions. The programme is designed so appointments, education, and networking work together.

## The shape of the hosted buyer's day

<Steps>
  <Step title="Day one: arrive and open">
    **Regiception** (combined registration and reception), **first-timer orientation** at flagships, a welcome education session, the hosted buyer's **first appointment block**, and an **opening reception** hosted by the destination.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Day two: the working day">
    Breakfast and education, a morning appointment block, lunch and learn, an afternoon appointment block, then an **open night** in the host city.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Day three: close out">
    Grab-and-go breakfast and a final appointment block. Flagships add a closing concert or the annual Connect 5K.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Regional events compress this into two days. Flagships extend it with tradeshow experiences, speaker sessions, and hosted meals.

## Appointment blocks

<Info>
  Flagship appointment blocks run **10:00-12:00 and 15:00-17:00** on days two and three. Regional blocks are similar in shape but shorter overall.
</Info>

- The hosted buyer's schedule is published in the event app before the event
- Each appointment is one-to-one with a specific supplier, in a fixed slot and location
- Meetings are short and focused; keep to the clock
- **Attendance is tracked**; check-ins at the hosted buyer's appointments feed into the hosted buyer's deposit compliance
- If a supplier is a no-show, report to the concierge desk so it does not count against the hosted buyer

<Warning>
  Missing appointments forfeits the hosted buyer's deposit and affects future eligibility. If something goes wrong, contact the concierge desk immediately rather than skipping.
</Warning>

## Beyond the appointments

The programme intentionally schedules soft time around the hard blocks:

- **Hosted breakfasts and lunches** are curated by track and are excellent for deeper conversations with suppliers or peers
- **Opening receptions** are hosted by the destination and are the best introduction to the host city
- **Open night** in the host city is unstructured; the hosted buyer chooses how to spend it
- **Education sessions** provide CMP-credit content (up to 14 to 15 hours at flagships)
- **Wellness activations** like the Connect 5K, Morning Walk Club (07:00 at Spring Marketplace), and quiet or prayer rooms are part of the standing programme

## Standing commitments across every event

- **Code of conduct**: zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or violence at any live or digital touchpoint
- **Sustainability**: reusable water bottles, recycling, locally sourced food, food donation to local charities
- **Quiet and prayer rooms** available onsite
- **Governing law**: New York, per Terms & Conditions, regardless of event location

## Onsite support

- **Concierge / help desk**: schedule changes, appointment issues, deposit questions
- **Registration**: badge pickup and issues
- **Hosted Buyer Experience Team**: the hosted buyer's paired contact is onsite for the duration
- **Mid-event cancellation**: `connect.cancellations@informa.com`

<Tip>
  If a hosted buyer finishes an appointment block early, encourage continued participation in the between-block moments such as coffee, walk-and-talks, and hosted lunches.
</Tip>
