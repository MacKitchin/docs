## Staff guidance

Use this split before sending a reply. Verify the event and registrant type before restating coverage or cancellation.

## Customer-safe information

- Published eligibility from [Who qualifies](/hosted-buyer-support/eligibility)
- Published coverage from [What is covered](/hosted-buyer-support/coverage)
- Event facts from the [Event calendar](/events/calendar/overview) after checking Known issues
- Appointment expectations on [Hosted buyer commitments](/hosted-buyer-support/commitments)
- Approved application guidance and FAQ short answers

## Internal-only context

- Salesforce and Visit IDs
- Webhook routing and sync defects
- Internal confidence scores
- Private source locations
- Unresolved ownership gaps

## Limits and escalation

Do not forward a full Hosted Buyer Support page to a customer. See [Exceptions and escalation](/hosted-buyer-support/exceptions-and-escalation).
