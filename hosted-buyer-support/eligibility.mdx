Hosted buyer eligibility is organised by **track**. Different Connect events run different tracks; a planner who fits one may not fit another.

## The general baseline

Before any track-specific criteria, Connect looks for:

- **50 or more rooms on peak nights** across the events the hosted buyer sources
- Ability to **rotate destinations** nationally or regionally rather than always sourcing the same city
- **Two-year verifiable event history**

The rotation requirement matters because destinations sponsor the room. A planner who is only ever going to book their home city has limited value to a national destination roster.

## Who Connect is trying to attract

From the flagship Why Sponsor page:

- **61%** of hosted buyers manage budgets over \$250K
- **31%** manage \$500K or more
- **34%** generate 1,000 or more room nights per event
- **45%** require 250 or more peak rooms
- **22%** require 500 or more

If the planner's programme is comparable to these examples, the planner may fit the profile.

## Regional versus flagship qualification

Regional events (Connect Texas, West, South \+ Faith, Winter Marketplace, Connect North) apply the same general baseline as the flagship but usually add a **geography filter**:

- **Connect Texas**: located in Texas
- **Connect West**: located in Western states
- **Connect South \+ Faith**: no strict geography, but Faith Track eligibility applies
- **Connect Winter Marketplace**: association and corporate planners based in the DC and NY areas, plus national destinations
- **Connect North**: located in or sourcing for the Midwest and Northeast

<Tip>
  If a planner meets the general bar but not a specific regional geography filter, staff should recommend a more suitable regional or the flagship. Do not stretch a track fit; the vetting team makes the final decision.
</Tip>

## What disqualifies applicants

- No two-year verifiable event history
- Sourcing volume well below 50 rooms on peak with no clear growth trajectory
- Purely local sourcing with no ability or intent to rotate
- Supplier or agency roles rather than planner roles (agencies may qualify if they are sourcing on behalf of an end client with authority)
