## Staff guidance

Escalate rather than invent a URL, refund, or travel promise.

| Exception | Goes to |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility exception | Hosted Buyer Experience — Rachel Piper's area |
| Deposit dispute or cancellation exception | Confirm event policy first, then human owner on Who owns what |
| Billing or invoice | Stephen Han (Finance) |
| Complaint | Named account or programme owner |
| Named attendee or buyer contact list | Do not share. Brand-and-title samples only |

## Customer-safe information

That the request has been routed, and any already-published eligibility, coverage, or event fact you have verified.

## Internal-only context

Visit and Salesforce identifiers, webhook defects, and dual cancellation-policy wording.

## Limits and escalation

Do not invent a public application URL. Two hosted-buyer cancellation policies are published across the portfolio — report both; do not pick a winner.
