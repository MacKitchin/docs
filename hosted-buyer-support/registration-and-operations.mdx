## Lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Application in Visit Create">
    The planner applies through the Visit Create form for a specific expo. Customer-facing application URLs remain unconfirmed in this knowledge base—do not invent one. A refundable **\$200** deposit is collected as the no-show control.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Webhook into connect-prod">
    Visit sends a GET notification to the Salesforce Site endpoint `/services/apexrest/visit/webhook`. `VisitWebhookReceiver` pulls changed visitors, then enqueues `VisitRegistrationProcessor`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hosted Buyer type gate">
    The processor skips anything that is not registration type `1ze3uz619` (Hosted Buyer). It looks up `Marketplace__c` by `Visit_Expo_Id__c`. If that field is blank on the marketplace, processing errors with "No Marketplace__c found for Visit Expo ID."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact and Account match, then Registration upsert">
    Contact match is tiered: email → first+last name → first+last+company, then create (Hosted Buyer contact record type) under a matched or created Account (`Unknown Hosted Buyer Company` fallback). Registration dedup is marketplace-scoped: `Visit_Visitor_Id__c`+`Visit_Expo_Id__c` → `Registrant__c`+`Marketplace__c` → email+`Marketplace__c`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Human qualification">
    Hosted Buyer Experience Managers vet role, two-year event history, sourcing scale, and rotation. Track bars are on [Business model](/company/business-model). Approved buyers are paired with an HBX contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Travel, hotel, appointment portal">
    From the FAQ library: TripEasy sends the flight invite (`travelnoreply@tripeasy.com`); `connectmeetings@its.com` is the travel team. Hotel is booked via the provided link. Appointment-portal access opens closer to the event. Which platform (Map Your Show / MatchPoint / custom) applies to each event is still unconfirmed — leave that TODO.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Onsite appointments, then deposit">
    Appointments are pre-scheduled. Completing the assigned schedule is the condition for returning the deposit. Only `Registration_Stage__c = 'Fully Registered'` counts as confirmed attendance in metrics.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## `Registration_Stage__c`

From the Salesforce KB Record Types page.

| Stage | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Registering | Initial form started |
| Pending Confirmation | Awaiting qualification or approval |
| Registered | Confirmed, not all steps complete |
| **Fully Registered** | All requirements met (travel booked, and so on). **This is the attendance count.** |
| Verbal | Verbal commitment, not yet formally registered |
| Cancelled / Declined / Stopped / Date Conflict | Terminal |

Visit maps `registrationState` into **Cancelled / Pending Confirmation / Registered**. Fully Registered is a later human or process step, not the raw webhook state.

Also on the record: `Hosted_Buyer_Type__c` (Anchor Planner, End User, Third Party, Non-Hosted Buyer), `Qualification_Status__c`, Visit keys (`Visit_Registration_Key__c`, `Visit_Visitor_Id__c`, `Visit_Order_Id__c`, `Visit_Expo_Id__c`), `Registrant__c`, `Planner_Account__c`, `Marketplace__c`, `Meeting_Event__c` (Campaign).

`Registration__c` has **237 fields** (225 custom). Do not invent a field. Use `sf sobject describe` if staff need a field that is not listed here.

## Deposit — do not collapse the versions

| Source | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| Customer FAQ (confidence 0.85) | Refund **check** at the conclusion of 1:1 appointments; must attend **all** scheduled 1:1s |
| Archived VIP playbook | Refunded **on-site** at the end of the event; example \$200 |
| Public [qualification](/hosted-buyer-support/application-and-approval) page | Full refund on completing the assigned schedule; 30+ day cancel = refund or credit; 7 days or fewer = forfeit plus one hotel night |
| Public Terms & Conditions (see Known issues) | 57+ days: refund minus 10% admin; within 56 days or no-show: full fee forfeited |

Report the version that matches the registrant type and the event page. Do not average them.

## Invite transfers

FAQ and playbook: transfers to a qualified colleague (typically director-level or above) require **rep approval**. Reps phrase this as having received approval to transfer the invite.

## What can go wrong in the sync

- Marketplace missing `Visit_Expo_Id__c`.
- Name-based Account matching creating near-duplicate Accounts when company strings vary.
- Processor runs in the Salesforce Site (guest) context.
- Monitor `Visit_Webhook_Log__c`, `Visit_Webhook_Activity__c`, and Setup → Apex Jobs.

Do not put Visit API keys, named-credential passwords, or guest-user permission sets on this page. The Named Credential is `Visit_API`.
