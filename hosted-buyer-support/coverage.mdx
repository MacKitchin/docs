## What Connect covers

Coverage recorded across the current hosted-buyer material includes:

- **Roundtrip airfare** through the approved booking route, or the published event-specific stipend where that model applies
- **Hotel accommodation** for the official event nights
- **Official programme meals and refreshments**
- **Event registration, education, and networking access** included in the hosted-buyer programme
- **Pre-scheduled supplier appointments** and the related appointment-platform access

<Note>
  These are programme-level reference points, not a universal travel authorisation. Confirm the event, approved booking route, covered nights, and any cap before advising the hosted buyer.
</Note>

## Special cases

### International (RTO) buyers

International buyers attending the flagship as receptive tour operators receive a **flight stipend up to \$600 per company** rather than direct flight booking. At Spring Marketplace, qualified RTOs receive complimentary hosted buyer registration.

### Informa colleagues

Informa PLC colleagues attending under the Informa track have registration fees waived but travel and hotel arrangements follow standard Informa T&E policy, not the hosted buyer package.

### Regional versus flagship

Coverage is broadly the same across regional and flagship events, but the shape differs:

| Item | Flagship | Regional |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hotel nights covered | Typically **3 nights** | Typically **2 nights** |
| Education content | Up to 14 to 15 hours CMP-credit | 3\+ hours |
| Appointments | Up to 45 (flagship) | Up to 25 (typical regional) |
| Signature moments | Multiple, including closing concert or Connect 5K | Destination-hosted opening reception, open night |

### Club Connect

Some regional events run **Club Connect**, a points-based hosted buyer rewards programme. Points can be redeemed for perks such as priority seating, hospitality-suite access, and airport-hotel transportation. Ask the assigned Hosted Buyer Experience contact whether the applicable event includes it.

## Cancellation and its cost

<Warning>
  Hosted-buyer cancellation windows and refund rules are published differently across event sites. Always confirm the specific cutoff and any late-cancel fee before advising on non-refundable travel or hotel extensions.
</Warning>

As a general reference:

- **30 or more days** before the event: full refund or credit
- **Inside 7 days** of the event: no refund plus a one-night hotel fee
- **Deposit** returned on completing the appointment schedule; forfeited on no-shows
