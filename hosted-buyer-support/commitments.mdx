Hosted attendance is an exchange: Connect provides the published event support and the hosted buyer completes the applicable programme commitments.

## Commitments staff should explain

- Provide accurate role, organisation, sourcing, and event-history information during application.
- Submit supplier requests and maintain a useful sourcing profile by the applicable deadline.
- Attend the event and complete the assigned appointment schedule.
- Use onsite support when a supplier no-shows or an unavoidable issue affects attendance.
- Follow the applicable cancellation and transfer process rather than abandoning the schedule.

## Deposit relationship

The current content consistently describes a **\$200 refundable deposit** as the appointment-attendance control. Source material conflicts on cancellation windows and does not confirm one universal return timing or mechanism.

<Warning>
  Staff must not promise a refund date, mechanism, or exception. Confirm the event-specific policy and escalate disputes through the Hosted Buyer Experience team.
</Warning>
