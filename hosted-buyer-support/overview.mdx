Use this section when advising or supporting an event planner through the hosted-buyer lifecycle. It explains the programme while preserving internal qualification, system, and escalation context.

<Info>
  **Audience:** Connect staff and approved internal agents. This is not a public hosted-buyer guide. Staff may adapt customer-safe facts, but must not share internal IDs, webhook details, unresolved source conflicts, or unverified policy.
</Info>

## What staff should explain

Connect covers the published costs of attendance in exchange for the hosted buyer completing a **committed appointment schedule with suppliers**. In practical terms:

- Roundtrip airfare, hotel for the event nights, and meals are covered
- All networking events and the education programme are included
- The hosted buyer takes a full schedule of one-to-one appointments matched to documented sourcing needs
- A **\$200 refundable deposit** is held and returned when the assigned appointments are completed, subject to the applicable event policy

<Warning>
  The deposit is the attendance-control mechanism for the programme. Missed appointments may forfeit the deposit and affect future eligibility.
</Warning>

## Hosted-buyer profiles covered

Event planners with real sourcing authority for group business:

- **Corporate** meeting and event planners
- **Association** meeting professionals
- **Sports** event organisers
- **Specialty** planners: social, military, and fraternal groups
- **Faith-based**, multicultural, LGBTQ\+, and women's group planners
- **International (RTO)** receptive tour operators booking inbound North American travel
- **Business travel** buyers with meaningful travel-management budgets

If the person represents an organisation selling services into the events industry, use [Exhibitor Support](/exhibitor-support/overview) instead.

## The baseline bar

Across most tracks and events, the general qualification is:

- **50 or more rooms on peak nights** for the events the hosted buyer sources
- Ability to **rotate destinations** nationally or regionally
- A **two-year verifiable event history**

Some tracks stack additional requirements on top - budget size, seniority, or event scale. Full detail is on [Application and qualification](/hosted-buyer-support/application-and-approval).
