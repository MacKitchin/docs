## Staff guidance

Use the [hosted buyer and attendee](/welcome/customer-faqs#hosted-buyer-and-attendee) pairs on the Customer FAQs extract. Prefer the **Short answer**. Surface confidence below 0.8. Stop when **When not to use** applies. Confirm event facts on the [Event calendar](/events/calendar/overview).

## Customer-safe information

The Short answer field, quoted rather than paraphrased into a new policy.

## Internal-only context

Frequency, rank, confidence scores, When-not-to-use annotations, and deposit-process discrepancies that must be reported as both versions.

## Limits and escalation

Deposit disputes, cancellation exceptions, billing, complaints, named attendee data, legal terms, and unverified travel promises require human escalation.
