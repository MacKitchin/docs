# Product brief

## Description

Connect Company Brain is the internal reference for Connect Meetings & Events. It gives staff and approved internal agents a navigable, retrieval-friendly view of the business, people, events, commercial products, audience-support playbooks, systems, data, automation, projects, and governance.

The site is a derived mirror. Salesforce `connect-prod`, Notion Company OS, and the Live Events wiki remain the systems of record for the facts they own.

## Primary audience

The primary readers are Connect Meetings & Events employees, new hires, operational teams, sales teams, and approved internal agents.

The Exhibitor Support and Hosted Buyer Support sections are internal staff playbooks. They are not public guides. They may ground customer-facing agents, but every page must distinguish customer-safe information from internal-only context and escalation rules.

## Jobs to be done

1. Find a reliable event, product, process, owner, system, metric, or policy without knowing the organisation's structure in advance.
2. Support suppliers and hosted buyers consistently while knowing what staff may communicate, what remains internal, and when to escalate.
3. Trace a statement back to its canonical source, owner, status, and review cadence.
4. Work safely with Salesforce, Visit Create, integrations, AI agents, and automation without exposing record data or secrets.
5. Understand active projects and known issues without mistaking planned or Working material for current policy.

## Motivation

Business knowledge currently spans Salesforce, Notion, the Live Events wiki, source repositories, and public event sites. The Company Brain reduces search time and supports reliable human and agent retrieval while preserving source ownership, unresolved conflicts, and access boundaries.

## Assumptions to verify

- The published Company Brain will be protected as an internal resource. Repository configuration can discourage external indexing, but authentication must be enforced by the hosting layer.
- The current content inventory is the approved scope. New factual pages should not be invented solely to fill a navigation slot; unverified gaps remain explicitly marked.
