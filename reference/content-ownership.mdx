## Staff guidance

Every substantive page should identify:

1. **Purpose** — why an employee would use the page.
2. **Audience** — the staff roles for whom it is relevant.
3. **Canonical source** — the system or document that owns the facts.
4. **Owner** — the person or team responsible for maintenance.
5. **Source status** — Canonical, Working, or Deprecated.
6. **Last reviewed** — including the expected review cadence.
7. **Staff guidance** — what employees should do.
8. **Customer-safe information** — what may be communicated externally.
9. **Internal-only context** — operational details that must remain internal.
10. **Limits and escalation** — what staff must not promise, disclose, or decide.
11. **Related pages** — links to the next likely staff task.

Use the [knowledge article template](/reference/knowledge-article-template) when creating a page.

## Customer-safe information

Customer-safe wording within a page does not make the complete page suitable for public distribution or direct customer access.

## Internal-only context

Working and Deprecated labels, ownership gaps, and review cadences.

## Limits and escalation

Do not present Working material as policy. Reconciliation of conflicts is a human decision by the source owner.
