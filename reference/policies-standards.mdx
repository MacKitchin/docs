These standards govern this knowledge base. They do not claim to replace HR, Legal, Finance, privacy, or information-security policies that have not been imported into the current source set.

## Knowledge-base standards

- Write for Connect staff and approved internal agents.
- Use British spelling, active voice, sentence-case headings, and concrete language.
- Give each factual page one canonical navigation home; link to it from related sections.
- Use root-relative internal links without file extensions.
- Preserve TODO markers for unverified facts rather than filling gaps.
- Use unique page titles for search, with shorter `sidebarTitle` values where useful.
- Add new pages to `docs.json`, then validate navigation and links.

## Content ownership and review

Every substantive page should identify or make discoverable:

1. Internal purpose and intended staff roles
2. Canonical source
3. Owner
4. Canonical, Working, or Deprecated status
5. Source or review date
6. Staff action
7. Customer-safe information where relevant
8. Internal-only context
9. Limits and escalation route
10. Related next task

## Customer-support standards

- Exhibitor Support and Hosted Buyer Support are internal staff playbooks.
- Customer-facing agents may ground on approved FAQs, knowledge articles, and event sources.
- Staff must not share internal IDs, defects, ownership gaps, private source locations, or named customer records.
- Billing, invoices, legal, contracts, complaints, specific discounts, and unpublished rates require human escalation.
- Never share a full attendee or buyer contact list.

## Data-governance standards

See [Data quality and governance](/operations/data/data-governance) for the operating controls. The core rules are to respect the owning system, keep record data out of MDX, use canonical metric definitions, and preserve unresolved conflicts.

## AI and agent standards

See [Staff and agent guardrails](/operations/ai/guardrails) and [Agent retrieval rules](/operations/ai/agent-retrieval). Agents must retrieve before answering, cite the page used, disclose weak source status, and stop at escalation boundaries.
