## Staff guidance

Copy the block below. Every step should name the evidence of completion.

## Customer-safe information

Not applicable unless the SOP itself is customer-facing — most are not.

## Internal-only context

Template only.

## Limits and escalation

Do not publish an SOP that implies a customer commitment.
