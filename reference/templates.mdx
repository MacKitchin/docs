## Project template

Project pages follow the Company OS structure:

1. Overview
2. Goals and success criteria
3. Key decisions
4. Next steps
5. Risks and blockers
6. Related documentation

The Master Projects row remains authoritative for owner, stage, priority, area, and timeline.

## Support-page extension

Exhibitor and hosted-buyer support pages must explicitly distinguish:

- what staff should do;
- what staff may communicate externally;
- what remains internal;
- what must be verified for the event or record;
- where exceptions are escalated.
