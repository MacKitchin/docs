## Staff guidance

Project pages follow this structure:

1. Overview
2. Goals and success criteria
3. Key decisions
4. Next steps
5. Risks and blockers
6. Related documentation

Do not treat a project page as current-state policy.

## Customer-safe information

Not applicable. Project pages are internal.

## Internal-only context

Stage, priority, and decision logs.

## Limits and escalation

The Master Projects row wins on owner, stage, priority, area, and timeline.
