## Contact page conflicts

<Warning>
  **Every event site publishes two different contact pages.** A `/contact-us/` page lists a fuller roster on `@informa.com` with general inquiries to `mac.kitchin@informa.com`. A separate `/contact/` page lists an overlapping but shorter roster on `@connectmeetings.com` with general inquiries to `info@connectmeetings.com`, and gives several of the same people different titles - Derek Rodriguez appears as both "EVP Sales" and "Sr VP Sales", Orlon Daniels as both "Senior Operations Leader" and "Senior Operations Manager", and the Hosted Buyer VP as both "Rachel Piper" and "Rachel Jackson".

  Verify which page is current before quoting any external contact.
</Warning>

Two further extraction issues on the Connect Texas page: a general inquiries address was captured as `mac.kitchen@informa.com`, which is almost certainly a misread of `mac.kitchin@informa.com`, and the "+ Medical" branding has no corresponding track or track page anywhere on the site.

## Cancellation policy conflicts

Every event publishes two different cancellation policies with no statement of which applies to whom.

| Source page | Policy |
| --- | --- |
| Hosted Buyers page | 30+ days: full refund or credit. 8-30 days with transportation booked: transportation may not be refundable. 7 days or fewer: no refund plus one hotel night fee |
| Terms & Conditions | 57+ days: refund minus 10% administration charge. Within 56 days or no-show: full fee forfeited |

They most likely apply to hosted buyers and to paying delegates respectively, but no page states that. Never quote one without establishing which registrant type you are dealing with.
