## Staff guidance

- Respect the owning system. This site is not a system of record.
- Keep record-level customer, visitor, and payment data out of MDX.
- Use canonical metric definitions on [Metrics and definitions](/operations/data/metrics).
- Preserve unresolved conflicts on [Known issues](/reference/known-issues). Do not pick a winner.

## Customer-safe information

Aggregate statistics and published package tables only. Never a full attendee or buyer contact list.

## Internal-only context

SOQL, object API names, known Salesforce defects, and unowned-area notes.

## Limits and escalation

Do not copy webhook URLs that contain secrets. Hosted-buyer dataset questions go to Bill Mathis. Schema questions go to Mac Kitchin.
