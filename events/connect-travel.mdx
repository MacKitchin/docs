The [Events, Travel and Sports](https://informaconnect.com/events-travel-sports/) hub on `informaconnect.com` is a topic portal, not a single calendar. It splits into four verticals. Meetings and Sports are already covered on the [regional](/events/regional) and [sports](/events/sports-and-summits) pages. **Destination Marketing and Strategy** is the vertical that was missing from this site: two Connect Travel events, owned by Ashleigh Trout.

Captured from the live event sites on **12 August 2026**. These are not hosted-buyer marketplaces for event planners. The hosted audience is **destination marketers** (DMOs and CVBs).

<Note>
  Both events sit on the same dates as a Connect marketplace flagship or regional, at a neighbouring or identical venue. The sites do not say "co-located" in so many words. The registration copy and hotel-block URLs do.
</Note>

## eTourism Marketplace 2026

<Info>
  **18-20 November** - Hilton West Palm Beach, Palm Beach. The hero does not print a year. Logo assets, the 27 October supplier room-block close, and the hotel booking URL (`connect-winter-supplier`) all match [Connect Winter Marketplace 2026](/events/regional).
</Info>

An appointment marketplace for destination marketers and the suppliers who sell to them - mar-tech, ad-tech, data, agencies, and other travel-marketing vendors. Format is the reverse-tradeshow mechanic aimed at DMOs rather than event planners: AI-matched nine-minute one-to-ones, education, and networking.

### How to attend

| Path | Who | What is covered | Published price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hosted Destination Marketer** | DMO, director-level or above, or with direct purchasing authority. Not an agency, media, or mar-tech/ad-tech supplier. | Complimentary roundtrip travel, hotel, waived registration. Up to 35 one-to-ones with travel-industry suppliers. Deposit typically \$99-\$200, refunded after completing the assigned appointments. | \$0 once approved |
| **Travel industry supplier** | Mar-tech, ad-tech, or other vendor whose primary business is selling a service to brands and businesses. | Up to 35 one-to-ones with DMO leaders, education, networking. Speed-dating format. | **\$6,250** |

Supplier hotel room block closes **27 October**. Same close date as Connect Winter Marketplace.

### Hosted DMO cancellation

Published on the Hosted DMO programme page. Same shape as the hosted-buyer policy on Connect events:

- 30+ days, before transport is booked: full refund or credit to a later event
- 30 days or fewer, transport booked: deposit may not be refunded; cancel transport through eTourism's Travel Services Manager
- 7 days or fewer: no refund, plus one hotel night fee

## eTourism Summit 2027

<Info>
  **21-23 April 2027** - Wynn Las Vegas, Las Vegas, NV. Same dates and hotel as [Spring Marketplace 2027](/events/connect-marketplace).
</Info>

A destination-marketing conference rather than an appointment marketplace. Positioning on the homepage: _Where Innovation Meets Inspiration_. "300+ marketing innovators." Registration includes **unlimited access to the Connect Spring Marketplace tradeshow floor**, which is the strongest published evidence that the two events share a campus.

It also runs the **eTSY Awards** and **Emerging Tourism Stars** programmes.

### How to attend

Published on `/how-to-attend/` for the 2027 edition.

| Path | Who | Includes | Published price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Marketers** | Attractions, destinations (DMOs, CVBs, TDCs), hotels, state or regional travel associations | Sessions, daily networking, event app, Spring Marketplace tradeshow floor | Early bird **\$1,600** with code `TRAV1`. Basic **\$1,895** |
| **Travel industry suppliers** | Marketing agencies, tech companies, and other travel-sector vendors | Same, plus Spring Marketplace access across tracks | Early bird **\$3,100** with code `TSUM1`. Basic **\$3,495** |

These are paid conference badges, not hosted packages. Do not describe Summit attendees as hosted buyers.

### Sponsors (2027 site)

Platinum: Ad+genuity, Steller, Ampersand, Atmosphere TV. Bronze: digilant. Sponsorship inquiries to `ashleigh.trout@informa.com`.

## How these relate to the Connect calendar

| Connect Travel event | Dates on the live site | Sits alongside | Evidence of overlap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| eTourism Marketplace | 18-20 November, Palm Beach, Hilton West Palm Beach | Connect Winter Marketplace 2026 at Palm Beach County Convention Center | Identical dates; supplier hotel block URL is `connect-winter-supplier`; same 27 October block close |
| eTourism Summit 2027 | 21-23 April 2027, Wynn Las Vegas | Spring Marketplace 2027 at The Wynn | Identical dates and hotel; Summit badge includes the Spring Marketplace tradeshow floor; FAQ directs people who reach Spring Marketplace to walk down the escalators to Bandol Ballroom / Reg Desk A |

<Tip>
  A supplier asking "which Palm Beach event do I buy?" is asking a real question. Winter Marketplace sells appointment packages to hospitality suppliers meeting event planners. eTourism Marketplace sells a \$6,250 supplier pass to vendors meeting DMO marketers. They are not interchangeable, even if they share a city and a week.
</Tip>
