## Event Portfolio Structure

Connect Meetings organizes events along several dimensions:

- **Brand**: Connect, BizBash, TSNN
- **Type**: Marketplace, Summit, Forum
- **Frequency**: Annual, Quarterly, Regional series
- **Audience**: Hospitality suppliers, meeting planners, hosted buyers

For event-specific dates, venues, and pricing, see the [Event Overview](/events/overview) page or the live event listings on the Connect website.
