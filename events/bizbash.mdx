This is the missing BizBash live event. It is a conference — case studies, masterclasses, panels — not a reverse tradeshow. It occupies the same building as [Connect Marketplace 2026](/events/connect-marketplace), which opens the next morning (24-26 August).

Badge pickup starts **13:00 on 23 August**. Plan Your Visit links a "Regiception" page, same vocabulary as the Connect events.

The other two BizBash live events remain on [Sports and summits](/events/sports-and-summits).

## Who it is for

Application-only passes, published on How to Attend. Standard passes have **no published price**. Premium passes say contact Michela Giovannotto.

| Pass | Reserved for | What the page says is included |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Brand Experience Pass** | In-house brand event and marketing professionals only | All sessions; brand-side networking; access to solution providers |
| **Standard Supplier Pass** | Event-industry suppliers | All sessions; logo on the event website; networking with in-house event leaders |
| **Independent Producer Pass** | Sole proprietor, LLC, or independent contractor, not full-time at a brand, agency, venue, or supplier | All sessions; networking with brands, agencies, and other independents |
| **Prime / Elite / Premier** | Sold as sponsorship | One registration plus a commercial slot, panel seat, or sponsored case study. Logo on the site. **Attendee list** (names, companies, emails) emailed two weeks after the event |

<Warning>
  Agents must still never share an attendee or buyer contact list. The Premier-tier "attendee list" is a sold sponsorship deliverable, not something a chatbot may quote, preview, or invent.
</Warning>
