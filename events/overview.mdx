## Classification rules

| Dimension | Use |
| --- | --- |
| **Brand** | Connect, Connect Travel, BizBash, or TSNN |
| **Format** | Marketplace, summit, forum, or programme track |
| **Audience** | Suppliers, hosted buyers, destination marketers, sports-event professionals, or media audiences |
| **Commercial route** | Appointment package, sponsorship, media, amplification, or registration |

<Warning>
  Event dates, venues, eligibility, appointment counts, and cancellation policy come only from the event pages or the Live Events wiki they mirror. Do not reconstruct them from project pages, CRM names, or marketing snippets.
</Warning>
