Two co-located events sit outside the marketplace format. They are conference-and-community events rather than appointment marketplaces, they use application-based passes rather than published package pricing, and they run out of the BizBash side of the business.

## Sports Innovation Forum 2026

<Info>
  **28-30 October 2026** - The Woodlands Waterway Marriott Hotel & Convention Center, The Woodlands, TX. Three days.
</Info>

Three days focused on how sports marketing, planning, and events actually get done - decision-makers, the approaches they are putting into practice, and connections across the sports industry. Cutting-edge education, peer-to-peer learning, and high-energy networking rather than scheduled one-to-ones.

### Pass types

All three are application and approval based, with no publicly listed price.

| Pass | For |
| --- | --- |
| **Brand Experience Pass** | Brand-side event and marketing professionals - retail, F&B, travel, CPG, automotive, banking, entertainment, sports |
| **Industry Supplier Pass** | Experiential agencies, mar-tech and ad-tech, DMOs and CVBs, vendor and solution providers |
| **Free Agent Pass** | Independent event professionals - sole proprietor, LLC, or independent contractor, not employed full-time by a brand, agency, venue, or supplier |

Premium sponsorship passes (Prime, Elite, Premier) are priced through the sales team only. Inquiries to `bizbash.sales@informa.com`.

### Community and sponsors

Fifteen sports organisations sit in the partner roster: NFL Players Association, Minnesota Vikings Football, Major League Rugby, New York City FC, San Jose Sharks, Athletes Unlimited, Dallas Mavericks, Houston Rockets, Delta Air Lines, Major League Soccer, United States Tennis Association, San Diego Wave FC, MLV NorCal, Barstool Sports, and On Location.

Sponsor tiers: Host is Visit The Woodlands. Premier - No Name Group, Czarnowski Collective, Wolfgang Puck Catering, Bluewater. Elite - Brand Activation Services, FG|PG, Gladiator Productions. Prime - AEG. Eleven partners including Brand Makers, SpotMyPhotos, Northern Lights Drone Shows, Quest Events, fielddrive, SponsorshipX, and Gather Voices.

### Programme themes

Sessions published at time of capture include Big Sports in the Big Apple, Innovations in Sponsorship Marketing, Women Leading the Future of Motorsports, Stealworthy VIP Experiences, The Women's Sports Report, and Houston Sports Ecosystem. Speakers include Brittany Stone (Ashley Global Retail), Heather Vaughan (San Jose Sharks), and Corey Kinder (Cleveland Browns).

## The Women's Summit 2026

<Info>
  **27-28 October 2026** - The Woodlands Waterway Marriott Hotel & Convention Center.
</Info>

An intimate forum for honest dialogue, shared perspective, and strategic insight on the state and future of women in sports. Sessions cover leadership, investment, and the path forward, with curated networking, an onsite happy hour, and a dinner in partnership with the headlining partner, Czarnowski Collective.

<Note>
  **Structurally important**: The Women's Summit is not separately ticketed. It runs as an **add-on track inside Sports Innovation Forum**. Attendees register for a Sports Innovation Forum pass - Brand Experience, Industry Supplier, or Free Agent - and select The Women's Summit as an add-on.
</Note>

### Programme

Day one runs a full afternoon and evening: HERstory in the Making, a Czarnowski-led session, Minute-To-Win-It, The Real Talk Roundtable on wellness, advocacy and growth, and Beyond Good Intentions on allyship, closing with happy hour and dinner. Day two is a shorter morning: breakfast roundtables, a fishbowl activity, More Than a Game on Houston's women's sports fandom, and a content round-up.

### Sponsors

Major League Rugby, Minnesota Vikings, Dallas Mavericks, NFLPA, United States Tennis Association, MLV NorCal, New York City FC, San Diego Wave FC, Athletes Unlimited, Houston Rockets.

## Venue context

The Woodlands is a master-planned destination founded in 1974, set in and around 28,000 tree-filled acres roughly 28 miles north of downtown Houston. Published distances vary by page - one states 20 miles from George Bush Intercontinental Airport and 30 miles from downtown Houston. Three of our events use this venue in a single October week.
