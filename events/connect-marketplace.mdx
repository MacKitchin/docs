Two events carry the flagship designation. They are materially larger than the regionals, run three days rather than two, and are the only ones with full track structures, speaker programmes, and published sponsorship rosters.

## Connect Marketplace 2026

<Info>
  **24-26 August 2026** (Monday to Wednesday) - Tampa Convention Center, Tampa Bay, FL - US Eastern time. Tagline: _Where Events Business Gets Done_. Value proposition: _High-Value Connections. Zero Wasted Time._ Public site: [informaconnect.com/connect-marketplace](https://informaconnect.com/connect-marketplace/).
</Info>

The flagship. 3,000\+ MICE professionals across three days of curated meetings, networking, and education. Host destination is Visit Tampa Bay. Closing night concert is the DJ duo Two Friends.

[BizBash Innovation Forum](/events/bizbash) occupies the same building on **23-25 August**, opening the afternoon before Marketplace. It is a BizBash conference, not a Marketplace track. Forum homepage: [informaconnect.com/bizbash-innovation-forum](https://informaconnect.com/bizbash-innovation-forum/).

### The six tracks

| Track | Scale | Who qualifies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Corporate | 400\+ | Corporate meeting planners. Example organisations: Cisco, DHL, Google, Mastercard, NHL, PNC, PWC, Subway |
| Association | 300\+ | Association meeting planners. Example organisations: American Psychological Association, CollegeBoard, Habitat for Humanity, National Kidney Foundation |
| Specialty | 300\+ (75\+ planners) | Social, military, and fraternal group planners |
| Sports | 200\+ | Competitive sporting events and sports-themed meetings. Examples: American Junior Golf Association, Spartan Race, USA Pickleball, USSSA, Varsity Spirit |
| International | 150\+ | Receptive tour operators booking inbound North America travel. Up to \$600 flight stipend per company |
| Informa | - | Informa PLC colleagues only, registration fees waived |

General buyer minimums across tracks: 50 or more peak rooms, ability to rotate nationally, and a two-year verifiable event history.

- **Appointment blocks**: 25 and 26 August, 10:00-12:00 and 15:00-17:00 each day

## Spring Marketplace 2027

<Info>
  **21-23 April 2027** - The Wynn, Las Vegas, Nevada. Over 2,000 event professionals. Public site: [informaconnect.com/connect-spring-marketplace](https://informaconnect.com/connect-spring-marketplace/).
</Info>

The spring flagship. Keynote is Jesse Itzler (Marquis Jet, Zico, co-owner of the Atlanta Hawks). Eleven speakers confirmed at time of capture with more expected, including Adam Sacks of Tourism Economics, Lisa Messina of Visit Orlando, Mike Satterfield of HelmsBriscoe, and Ken Holsinger of Freeman.

[eTourism Summit 2027](/events/connect-travel) publishes the same dates at the same hotel. Summit homepage: [informaconnect.com/etourism-summit](https://informaconnect.com/etourism-summit/). A Summit marketer or supplier badge includes unlimited access to this event's tradeshow floor. They are different products: this page is the MICE appointment marketplace; the Summit is a paid destination-marketing conference.

### Tracks

Six: Corporate, Association, Citywide, Business Travel, Diversity, and International (RTO Marketplace).

| Track | Qualification highlights |
| --- | --- |
| Corporate | 500\+ professionals expected; Director, VP, or C-suite with six to seven figure budgets |
| Association | 550\+ professionals; 50\+ rooms on peak |
| Citywide | 87% manage \$1M\+ budgets, 50% generate 2,500\+ rooms on peak; minimum 1,000\+ rooms or 100,000\+ sq ft |
| Business Travel | 1 in 3 buyers controls \$10M\+ travel budgets; 80%\+ manager level or above |
| Diversity | Community goal of 100\+ diversity event professionals; serves faith-based, fraternal, nonprofit, education, LGBTQ\+, multicultural, and women's group planners |
| International (RTO) | Receptive tour operators booking inbound US and Canada travel; complimentary hosted buyer registration for qualified RTOs |

### Signature moments

Regiception and first-timers orientation on day one; general session, two appointment blocks, open night, and the fifth annual Connect White Party on day two; invite-only hosted breakfasts and a final appointment block on day three. Morning Walk Club opens day two at 07:00.
