Five regional marketplaces run between October 2026 and February 2027. They share a common format - a compressed two- or three-day version of the flagship - and a common entry price of **\$4,250 for 12 supplier appointments**. Their websites are flatter than the flagship sites, with no dedicated speakers, tracks, or why-exhibit pages.

Homepages recaptured on **12 August 2026**. Dates, venues, and published prices were unchanged from the earlier crawl. Public URLs recaptured **27 August 2026** — see [Public event sites](/events/public-sites). New facts: Connect South now labels Association | Corporate | Faith on the hero; Connect North now names Canada in the catchment; Winter Marketplace body copy still places the event in "the nation’s capital." The Destination Marketing vertical on the same hub is documented separately under [Connect Travel events](/events/connect-travel).

## Connect Texas \+ Medical 2026

<Info>
  **28-29 October 2026** - The Woodlands Waterway Marriott Hotel & Convention Center, The Woodlands, TX. Two days. Public site: [informaconnect.com/connect-texas](https://informaconnect.com/connect-texas/).
</Info>

- **Hosted buyer**: complimentary. Roundtrip airfare, two nights hotel, all networking events, 3\+ hours education, AI-matched appointments. Qualification: located in Texas, generates 50\+ rooms on peak, able to rotate nationally or regionally.
- **Supplier**: from \$4,250 for 12 one-to-one appointments.
- **Club Connect**: the points-based hosted buyer rewards programme, redeemable for priority seating, hospitality suite access, and airport-hotel transportation.
- **Sponsors**: Visit The Woodlands (also hosts the opening night reception) and GroupSync. Sponsorship inquiries to `derek.rodriguez@informa.com`.
- **Room block closes**: 5 October.
- **Public resources**: [How to attend](https://informaconnect.com/connect-texas/how-to-attend/) · [Agenda](https://informaconnect.com/connect-texas/agenda/) · [Hosted buyers](https://informaconnect.com/connect-texas/hosted-buyers/) · [Plan your visit](https://informaconnect.com/connect-texas/plan-your-visit/) · [Sponsors](https://informaconnect.com/connect-texas/sponsors/) · [Marketing toolkit](https://informaconnect.com/connect-texas/marketing-toolkit/) · supplier [VisitCloud `1lnfom93345oj`](https://register.visitcloud.com/survey/1lnfom93345oj) · hosted buyer [`1xej4ozp2jlko`](https://register.visitcloud.com/survey/1xej4ozp2jlko) · hotel [idem.events/r/connect-texas-medical-attendee](https://www.idem.events/r/connect-texas-medical-attendee)

<Warning>
  The Plan Your Visit page describes Frisco, TX as the destination while every other page says The Woodlands. This is leftover template content from a different Connect event and has not been corrected at source. Also note the "\+ Medical" branding has no corresponding track or track page.

## Connect West 2026

<Info>
  **4-5 November 2026** - The Westin Kierland Resort and Spa, Scottsdale, AZ. Two days. Hashtag `#connectwest`. Public site: [informaconnect.com/connect-west](https://informaconnect.com/connect-west/).
</Info>

- **Hosted buyer**: roundtrip airfare to Scottsdale, two nights hotel, opening reception and networking, 3\+ hours education, up to 25 appointments. Qualification: located in Western states, 50\+ rooms on peak, able to rotate.
- **Supplier**: opening reception and networking, 3\+ hours education, up to 25 appointments with qualified hosted buyers.
- Appointments described as AI-powered and matched, in focused nine-minute meetings.
- **Signature moments**: pre-conference adventures in Scottsdale, Regiception, "Secret Stress Management Skills in the Sonoran Desert" welcome session, closing cocktails.
- **Room block closes**: 14 October. Badge pickup for returning attendees from 15:00 on 4 November. Photo service by Spot My Photos.

<Info>

## Connect Winter Marketplace 2026

<Info>
  **18-20 November 2026** - Palm Beach County Convention Center, Palm Beach, FL. Three days. Positioning: _The Marketplace Behind Meaningful Connections._ Public site: [informaconnect.com/connect-winter-marketplace](https://informaconnect.com/connect-winter-marketplace/).
</Info>

- **Target audience**: association and corporate planners based in the Washington DC area and New York, plus destinations, suppliers, and hospitality partners nationally.
- **Hosted buyer categories**: Association, Corporate, DC Hosted Buyer, New York Buyers. Homepage eyebrow: Association · Corporate · DC · New York.
- **Registration paths**: Hosted Buyer (travel and hotel covered, up to 45 appointments, 4\+ hours education); Supplier (up to 40 guaranteed appointments); Buyer Power Pass / non-hosted (access and education, no scheduled appointments).
- **Signature moments**: Regiception and happy hour appointments on day one; opening reception hosted by the Hilton West Palm Beach and Discover the Palm Beaches; open night in Palm Beach on day two; optional wellness activities on day three.
- **Room block closes**: 27 October.
- **Same week as** [eTourism Marketplace](/events/connect-travel) at the Hilton West Palm Beach ([etourism-marketplace](https://informaconnect.com/etourism-marketplace/)). Different audience (event planners vs DMO marketers). Different supplier price.
- **Public resources**: [How to attend](https://informaconnect.com/connect-winter-marketplace/how-to-attend/) · [Agenda](https://informaconnect.com/connect-winter-marketplace/agenda/) · [Hosted buyers](https://informaconnect.com/connect-winter-marketplace/hosted-buyers/) · [Sponsors](https://informaconnect.com/connect-winter-marketplace/sponsors/) · CTA labelled Hosted Buyer Registration [VisitCloud `3d5r46gen3aq5`](https://register.visitcloud.com/survey/3d5r46gen3aq5) · CTA labelled Supplier Registration [`25ss34wgg94rd`](https://register.visitcloud.com/survey/25ss34wgg94rd) (button labels and body copy on How to Attend disagree — see [Public event sites](/events/public-sites)) · hotel [idem.events/r/connect-winter-supplier](https://www.idem.events/r/connect-winter-supplier) (same hotel-block URL as eTourism Marketplace)

<Warning>
  No pricing is published for any tier on this event - do not assume it matches the flagship. The homepage hero still says "Connect Winter Marketplace **2027**" against 18-20 November **2026** dates. A body paragraph also says corporate and association professionals "will join in the nation’s capital" while every venue line is Palm Beach. Both are live. Do not reconcile.
</Warning>

## Connect North 2027

<Info>
  **22-24 February 2027** - Cincinnati, Ohio. Positioned as the official meeting place for event professionals in the North. Public site: [informaconnect.com/connect-north](https://informaconnect.com/connect-north/).
</Info>

- **Catchment** on the homepage: Midwest, Chicago, New England, the greater Northern U.S., and **Canada**.
- **Hosted buyer**: roundtrip airfare to Cincinnati, two nights hotel, all networking events, 3\+ hours education, appointments with qualified suppliers. Qualification: located in or sourcing events for the Midwest and Northeast, 50\+ rooms on peak, able to rotate nationally or regionally.
- **Supplier**: from \$4,250 for 12 appointments.
- **Speakers**: Violet Crabb (American Academy for Oral and Systemic Health), Savannah Montgomery (Salesforce), Peter Hackes (Performedia).
- **2025 hosted buyer organisations** give a sense of the room: Cisco, Emory University School of Medicine, Habitat for Humanity, American Academy of Family Physicians, Canvas Credit Union, National Association of Intercollegiate Athletics, Powerlifting America.
- Sponsorship inquiries to `sales@connectmeetings.com`.
- **Public resources**: [How to attend](https://informaconnect.com/connect-north/how-to-attend/) · [Agenda](https://informaconnect.com/connect-north/agenda/) · [Agenda download](https://informaconnect.com/connect-north/agenda/download/) · [Schedule at a glance](https://informaconnect.com/connect-north/schedule-at-a-glance/) · [Hosted buyers](https://informaconnect.com/connect-north/hosted-buyers/) · [Sponsors](https://informaconnect.com/connect-north/sponsors/) · 2025 media kit [Turtl](https://informa.turtl.co/story/events-or-travel-or-sports-media-kit-2025/page/1). The homepage did not expose VisitCloud survey URLs on 27 August 2026 capture.

<Warning>
  This event carries the most source conflicts in the portfolio. The agenda pages are headed January 2026 while every other page says 22-24 February 2027. The sponsors page lists Visit Pittsburgh as host, despite the homepage reading "Thank you, Pittsburgh - Cincinnati, you're up next", and the sponsors meta description references Connect North 2026. Three different date variants exist across the site. Nothing has been reconciled at source.
</Warning>
