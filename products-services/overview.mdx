## Commercial offer at a glance

The two source documents used for this section are the **Connect Marketplace 2026 sponsorship deck** and the **Events | Travel | Sports 2025 Media Kit**.

## Audience and network context

The 2025 media kit positions BizBash, TSNN, and Corporate Event News as a combined events-industry media network with **378K combined social followers**, **164K monthly unique visits**, **411K monthly owned-channel views**, and **93.8K newsletter subscribers**. It also cites a **\$1.1M average event budget** among the audience.

Brand-level figures in the kit include:

| Brand | Audience highlights |
| --- | --- |
| BizBash | 107K monthly uniques, 231K monthly views, 46.7K newsletter subscribers |
| TSNN | 53K monthly users, 175K monthly views, 42.9K total newsletter subscribers |
| Corporate Event News | Positioned around corporate event and meeting planners; no comparable traffic totals are published in the kit |

## How the offer fits together

The product portfolio can be sold as standalone placements or combined around an event lifecycle:

1. **Before an event** — sponsored social, eblasts, newsletters, pre-show email, content activation, retargeting, and event targeting.
2. **At the event** — trade-show booths, signage, hospitality, activations, branded attendee touchpoints, hosted-buyer programs, app sponsorships, push notifications, and custom video.
3. **After the event** — post-show email, event targeting, lead insights, retargeting, sponsored content, and other media that extends campaign reach.

See **Event sponsorships** for the Connect Marketplace 2026 inventory and **Media & amplification** for the cross-brand media portfolio.
