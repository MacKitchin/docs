## Media network

The 2025 Events | Travel | Sports Media Kit brings together **BizBash**, **Trade Show News Network (TSNN)**, and **Corporate Event News** as a combined events-industry media network. The kit cites **378K combined social audience**, **164K monthly unique visits**, **411K monthly owned-channel views**, and **93.8K newsletter subscribers** across the network.

## Email newsletters

Newsletter advertising is placed alongside editorial content and can use a leaderboard or client-provided advertorial content, subject to availability.

### BizBash

- **BizBash Buzz** — delivered three times weekly; media kit states **40K event pros for \$4,000**.
- **BizBash Sports** — delivered twice monthly; focuses on sports-event innovations and industry developments. The source kit does not publish a price for this newsletter.

### TSNN

- **The Industry This Week** — weekly trade-show industry newsletter; media kit states **13K event pros for \$4,000**.
- **Association Show News** — twice monthly; focuses on association-sector shows and industry associations. The source kit does not publish a price.

### Corporate Event News

- **Corporate Event News** — delivered Wednesdays, covering corporate event planning, marketing, destinations and venues, people and development, technology, and industry news; **\$4,000** to the corporate database.

## Relationship to Connect Marketplace sponsorship products

The Connect Marketplace 2026 deck includes overlapping event-amplification concepts — **Custom Video, Lead Insights, Digital Media, and Event Targeting** — but does not publish rates for those four items. The 2025 media kit provides starting prices for Custom Video, Lead Insights, and Event Targeting, and gives the broader paid/owned media rate card that can support a multi-channel event campaign.

Because the two documents are from different years, do not assume the 2025 media-kit rates automatically apply to a 2026 Connect Marketplace sponsorship proposal without current sales confirmation.
