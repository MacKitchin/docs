## Media network

The kit presents BizBash, Trade Show News Network (TSNN), and Corporate Event News as one events-industry media network.

Figures below are only those clearly labelled in the extracted 2026 PDF. The audience/social spread is layout-ambiguous. Do not total or average the split lines.

| Measure as labelled in the 2026 PDF | Figure |
| --- | --- |
| Digital users (2024) | 1.8M combined line; BizBash 1.3M, TSNN 430,000, CEN 55,000 |
