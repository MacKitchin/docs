The programme promotes a supplier story to the BizBash audience through native placements and allows the audience to be pixelled for later campaigns.

[Original PDF](/files/media-specs/content-activation.pdf)

## What to submit

- Story URL on the supplier's own site. UTM codes are allowed. **Destination redirects are not permitted**; the final URL must match the display URL.
- Headlines and description.
- Images and logos.
- Social handles and branded-content partner approvals.

## Copy and image limits

| Element | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| Headline (short) | 30 characters |
| Headline (long) | 90 characters |
| Description | 90 characters |
| Landscape image | 1200 x 628 px, JPG or PNG, 1.91:1, maximum 5120KB |
| Square image | 1200 x 1200 px, JPG or PNG, maximum 5120KB |
| Landscape logo | 1200 x 300 px, JPG or PNG, 4:1, maximum 5120KB |
| Square logo | 1200 x 1200 px, JPG or PNG, maximum 5120KB |

Avoid filters unless needed to sharpen a photograph.

The index sheet also lists a **Connect Content Activation** product. There is no separate Connect spec in this folder; do not invent one.
