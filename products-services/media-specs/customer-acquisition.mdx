Connect and BizBash recruit planners for a host destination. The sheet promises a **minimum of 10** high-qualified planners and event marketers, with recruitment, coordination, and travel expenses handled by Connect.

<Warning>
  Never quote these starting prices as a live offer without the sales owner. The sheet says “starting at” and “for an additional cost” for air and rooming lists.
</Warning>

## FAM trip recruiting

**Starting at \$25,000** on the sheet.

- Audit of meeting / exhibit space (hotels) or overall space (venue).
- Target planners by compatibility with the destination or hotel.
- Collect interest for host review before securing attendees.
- Air travel and a rooming list are **additional cost**.

## Destination focus groups

**Starting at \$30,000** on the sheet.

- Same space audit and planner review as a FAM.
- Pre-arrival survey with the host.
- Agenda designed to highlight the host.
- Two-hour facilitated session at the end of the programme.
- Post-arrival survey after planners return home.
- Detailed report within **14 days**: participants, pre-arrival results, session discussion, post-event survey.
