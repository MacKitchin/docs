A sponsored email has three parts: **subject line**, **HTML body**, and a **plaintext `.txt` version**. Materials are due **five business days** before the scheduled send. Production tests the message and sends a test for supplier approval before deployment.

<Warning>
  The 2025 sheet caps HTML width at **600 pixels**. The 2024 sheet says **600–728 pixels** and adds a **minimum height of 820 pixels**. Do not treat one as a silent replacement for the other until production confirms which sheet applies to the booking.
</Warning>

## HTML rules that both sheets share

[2025 PDF](/files/media-specs/dedicated-email-2025.pdf) · [2024 PDF](/files/media-specs/dedicated-email-2024.pdf)

- Submit a complete HTML file. Images, links, bold, and italics are allowed.
- Layers, background images, rollovers, forms, JavaScript, Flash, and CSS are prohibited.
- Personalisation is available (first and/or last name, with a default if the name is missing).
- Each image about 50KB, JPG or GIF, referenced by absolute URL. BizBash can host images if they are sent with the file.
- Do **not** submit Constant Contact or other copyrighted third-party templates; they are returned.
- Put the offer, call to action, dates, location, and links as **real text** in the top third. Many clients block images.
- HTML may be supplied as a link to a dedicated web page of the message. Images to be hosted go in a zip.

## Where the sheets diverge

| Rule | 2025 sheet | 2024 sheet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum HTML width | 600 pixels | 600–728 pixels |
| Minimum HTML height | Not stated | 820 pixels |
| Inbox | `bizbashprod@informa.com` | `bizbashprod@informa.com` or `connectprod@informa.com` |

## Subject line

Keep it short. Recipients may see only the first few words. Avoid “spammy” terms such as “Free!”, all caps, and heavy use of red or exclamation marks.

## Plaintext version

A `.txt` file with no images, hyperlinks, or styling. Type every URL in full, including `http://`.

## Best practices printed on the sheets

1. Call to action and click-through at the top (preview pane).
2. One offer.
3. Be succinct.
4. Assume mobile; more than half of email is opened on a phone. Responsive design if possible. Limit the subject line to about 30 characters.
5. Readable type and colour.
6. Production tests the code; the 2025 sheet names `http://www.contactology.com/check_mqs.php` as a spam-score example.
