## Standing rules across the folder

- Materials are due **five business days** before campaign start on every sheet that states a lead time.
- Rich media, HTML5, and non-standard units need **ten additional business days** on the onsite banner sheets.
- A **click-through URL** is required with all banner and display creative. UTM links are accepted on the offsite and native sheets.
- Flash is not accepted on any sheet.
- BizBash may reject creative that it considers illegal or otherwise inappropriate.
