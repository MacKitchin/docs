## Positions

An **Issue Sponsor** buys all positions and must supply **two different** display-box ads.

| Position | Name | Dimensions | Format |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Title sponsor | See copy rules | Text and link |
| B | Advertorial | See copy rules | JPEG plus text |
| C | First display box | 300 x 250 | JPEG, GIF |
| D | Second display box | 300 x 250 | JPEG, GIF |

A click-through link is required with all creative. Some third-party tracking tags interfere with BizBash link tracking; production will agree which tracker takes priority.

## Title sponsor copy

Maximum **100 characters including spaces**. Company name is spelled out as text; **logos are not accepted**. The click-through is embedded in the company name.

## Display boxes

- File size maximum **80KB**
- 300 x 250 pixels
- Hairline border on light-colour ads
- Animated GIF is allowed; the **first frame must stand alone** because some email clients do not animate
- Flash is not accepted

## Advertorial

- Image maximum **80KB**, **400 x 267** pixels
- Headline maximum **10 words**
- Body maximum **500 characters** (about 100 words), **single paragraph only**
- No logos
- Copy may be edited to fit and to match editorial style

## Submission

Email `production@bizbash.com`. Materials due **five business days** before the newsletter campaign.
