This guide walks you through everything you need to run your first meeting with ConnectMeetings. By the end, you will have an account, a connected Microsoft 365 workspace, a scheduled meeting, and a published agenda — ready to share with your team.

<Note>
  ConnectMeetings works best with a Microsoft 365 subscription, which enables calendar sync, Teams meeting links, and SharePoint document archiving. If your organization does not use Microsoft 365, you can still use ConnectMeetings in email-only mode — calendar invitations will be sent as standard `.ics` files instead.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [connectmeetings.ca](https://connectmeetings.ca) and click **Sign Up** in the top-right corner.

    Fill in your organization details:
    - **Organization name** — the name that will appear on agendas and minutes documents.
    - **Sector** — choose from municipal government, school board, healthcare, or other public sector.
    - **Plan** — select Starter, Professional, or Enterprise. You can upgrade at any time.

    Check your inbox for a verification email and click the confirmation link to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Microsoft 365">
    After logging in, navigate to **Settings > Integrations > Microsoft 365** and click **Connect**.

    You will be redirected to a Microsoft OAuth consent screen. Sign in with your Microsoft 365 admin account and grant the requested permissions. ConnectMeetings requests access to:
    - Outlook calendar (read and write) — to check availability and create calendar events.
    - Microsoft Teams — to generate Teams meeting links automatically.
    - SharePoint — to archive agenda and minutes documents to your chosen document library.

    Once authorized, you will be returned to ConnectMeetings with a green **Connected** status next to each service.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first meeting">
    From the dashboard, click **New Meeting**.

    Enter the following details:
    - **Title** — a short, descriptive name for the meeting (e.g., *Monthly Council Meeting — July 2025*).
    - **Date and time** — pick a date and start time. ConnectMeetings will show you a real-time availability summary for all invited attendees.
    - **Duration** — set an expected end time. This is used to block calendars and track whether meetings run over schedule.
    - **Attendees** — search for team members by name or email address and add them. Each attendee receives a role of Organizer or Participant.

    Click **Save Draft** to continue building the meeting before sending invitations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your agenda">
    Open the **Agenda** tab inside your new meeting and click **Add Item**.

    For each agenda item, you can:
    - Enter a title and a short description.
    - Assign a **presenter** responsible for leading that item.
    - Set a **time allocation** in minutes. ConnectMeetings will display a running total so your agenda stays within the meeting's scheduled duration.
    - Attach supporting documents directly from SharePoint or your local drive.

    Repeat until all items are covered. You can drag and drop items to reorder them at any time.

    <Tip>
      Save time on recurring meetings — such as weekly stand-ups or monthly board meetings — by saving your agenda as a **template**. Go to **Agenda > Save as Template**, give it a name, and reuse it next time with one click. Templates can include standard items like *Approval of Previous Minutes* and *Other Business* pre-populated.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send invitations">
    When your agenda is ready, click **Publish Agenda**. This locks the agenda for attendees to review before the meeting.

    Click **Send Invitations** to dispatch calendar invites to all attendees in one action. Each invitation includes:
    - The meeting title, date, time, and location or Teams link.
    - A link to the published agenda in ConnectMeetings.
    - An `.ics` calendar attachment compatible with Outlook and other calendar clients.

    Attendees can accept or decline directly from their calendar. Their responses are reflected in real time on your meeting dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
