ConnectMeetings turns your agenda into a live minutes document the moment a meeting starts. Every agenda item becomes a structured section ready for notes, motions, and votes. Multiple recorders can contribute simultaneously, so nothing is missed even in fast-moving sessions.

## Starting minutes

Open the meeting in ConnectMeetings and click **Start Minutes**. The editor opens with all agenda items pre-loaded as individual sections. The meeting is now in active status and the minutes record is open for input.

<Note>
The minutes document is permanently and automatically linked to the meeting record. You do not need to save or attach it separately — it will always be accessible from the meeting's **Minutes** tab, even years after the meeting concludes.
</Note>

## Real-time collaboration

Any participant with the Recorder or Organizer role can type in the minutes editor at the same time. Changes sync instantly across all open sessions — similar to collaborative document editing. Each contributor's cursor is color-coded so recorders can see where others are working and avoid overlap.

<Tip>
For formal meetings, assign a dedicated minute-taker using the **Recorder** role before the meeting starts. A single point of responsibility reduces conflicting edits and ensures a consistent voice throughout the document. Other participants can still add notes, but the designated recorder makes final decisions on wording.
</Tip>

## Recording agenda item notes

Each agenda item has its own collapsible notes section in the live editor. Click into a section and type freely. Notes support basic formatting — bold, italics, bullet lists, and numbered lists — so you can structure content clearly as the discussion unfolds.

Use the **Mark as Discussed** toggle on each item to indicate when the body has moved on. Discussed items collapse by default but remain fully editable.

## Capturing motions

To record a motion, click **Add Motion** inside any agenda item section. Fill in the motion form:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Mover** | Member who made the motion |
| **Seconder** | Member who seconded the motion |
| **Motion Text** | Exact wording of the motion as stated |
| **Vote Outcome** | Carried, Failed, Tabled, or Withdrawn |

Save the motion to embed it in the agenda item's minutes section. You can attach a roll call vote to any motion (see below).

## Roll call votes

For formal votes that require a recorded tally, click **Add Roll Call Vote** on a motion. ConnectMeetings displays the full list of voting members. For each member, select their vote:

- **Yes**
- **No**
- **Abstain**
- **Absent**

The tally — total Yes, No, Abstain, and Absent counts — is calculated and displayed automatically. The roll call is embedded in the minutes and included in all exported documents.

## Resolutions and decisions

When the body reaches a formal decision, click **Add Resolution** on the relevant agenda item. Enter the resolution number (if applicable) and the full resolution text. Mark the item as **Resolved** to visually flag it in the minutes. Resolved items are highlighted in the final published minutes document.

## In-meeting action items

You can create action items on the fly without leaving the minutes editor. Inside any agenda item section, click **Add Action Item** and fill in:

- **Title** — what needs to be done
- **Owner** — the person responsible
- **Due Date** — when it must be completed
- **Priority** — Low, Medium, or High

The action item is saved to the meeting's action item list and the owner is notified immediately by email. See [Track and Manage Meeting Action Items](/minutes/action-items) for full details on managing action items after the meeting.

## Finishing and submitting

When the meeting concludes, click **End Meeting** in the minutes editor. You are then prompted to **Submit Minutes for Review**. This sends the draft minutes to the designated reviewer (configured in Meeting Settings) and updates the meeting status to **Minutes Under Review**.

Submitted minutes follow the same review and approval flow as agendas. Once approved, minutes are published and accessible to all meeting participants.

## Full minutes recording flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the meeting and start minutes">
    Navigate to the meeting and click **Start Minutes**. The agenda pre-loads as structured sections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete roll call">
    Use the **Attendance** panel to mark each member as Present, Absent, or Arrived Late before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record notes for each item">
    As the meeting progresses, type discussion notes inside each agenda item's section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture motions and votes">
    Click **Add Motion** for any item that involves a formal motion. Add a **Roll Call Vote** if a recorded tally is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log resolutions and action items">
    Use **Add Resolution** for formal decisions and **Add Action Item** for tasks that arise during discussion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark items as discussed">
    Toggle **Mark as Discussed** on each item as the meeting moves through the agenda.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the meeting and submit minutes">
    Click **End Meeting**, then **Submit Minutes for Review** to send the draft minutes to your reviewer.
  </Step>
</Steps>
