Decisions made in meetings only move forward when someone is responsible for acting on them. ConnectMeetings action items give every task a named owner, a due date, and a visible status — so nothing agreed upon in a meeting slips through the cracks between sessions.

## What is an action item

An action item is a discrete task that arises from a meeting discussion or decision. Each action item in ConnectMeetings has the following fields:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Short description of the task |
| **Description** | Additional context, instructions, or background |
| **Owner** | The person responsible for completing the task |
| **Due Date** | The date by which the task should be complete |
| **Priority** | Low, Medium, or High |
| **Status** | Current state of the task (see statuses below) |

Action items are linked to the meeting and agenda item where they were created, providing full traceability back to the original discussion.

## Creating action items

You can create action items in two ways:

**During a live meeting:** Use the **Add Action Item** button inside any agenda item section of the minutes editor. This is the most common path — capturing tasks in the moment as they are assigned. See [Record Live Meeting Minutes](/minutes/live-minutes) for details on the minutes editor.

**After the meeting:** Open the meeting record, go to the **Action Items** tab, and click **New Action Item**. Use this to capture follow-up tasks identified during the review of minutes or during post-meeting discussions.

## Assigning owners

Every action item must have a single owner — the person accountable for completing it. Type the owner's name or email in the **Owner** field to search your organization's directory. As soon as you save the action item, ConnectMeetings sends the owner an email notification with the task details, due date, and a direct link to update their item.

<Note>
Action items are visible to the assigned owner and all users with the Organizer or Admin role. General attendees can only see action items where they are the named owner.
</Note>

## Statuses

Action items move through four statuses over their lifecycle:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Task has been created and assigned; work has not started |
| **In Progress** | Owner has begun working on the task |
| **Complete** | Task is finished |
| **Cancelled** | Task is no longer required |

Owners update their own item's status directly. Organizers and Admins can update the status of any item.

## The Action Items dashboard

The **Action Items** dashboard gives you a single view of every action item across all meetings in your organization. Access it from the left navigation bar under **Action Items**.

Use the filters to narrow the list:

- **Assignee** — see all tasks belonging to a specific person
- **Meeting** — view tasks from a single meeting
- **Date Range** — filter by due date window
- **Status** — focus on Open, In Progress, Complete, or Cancelled items

<Tip>
Open the Action Items dashboard at the start of each meeting to review outstanding items from previous sessions. Paste a summary into the agenda or address them verbally under a standing "Business Arising" item. This keeps accountability visible and prevents tasks from aging without follow-up.
</Tip>

## Updating status

**Owners** can update the status of their own action items at any time. Open the action item from the dashboard or from the email notification link, change the status, add an optional progress note, and click **Save**.

**Organizers and Admins** can update the status of any action item in their meetings. This is useful for marking items complete on behalf of an owner, or cancelling tasks that are no longer relevant after a policy change.

## Due date reminders

ConnectMeetings sends automated email reminders to action item owners on the following schedule by default:

| Reminder | Timing |
|---|---|
| First reminder | 7 days before the due date |
| Final reminder | 1 day before the due date |
| Overdue notice | 1 day after the due date (if not completed) |

Admins can adjust the reminder schedule in **Settings → Notifications → Action Item Reminders**. Reminders can be turned off for specific users or globally for the organization.

## Reporting

Export action items from the dashboard by clicking **Export → CSV**. The export includes all visible fields — title, description, owner, meeting, due date, priority, status, and creation date.

For summary analytics, go to **Reports → Action Items**. This view shows:

- **Completion rate** by meeting, organizer, or date range
- **Average time to completion** across your organization
- **Overdue items** broken down by owner or department

Use these reports to identify patterns — recurring owners with consistently overdue tasks, or meetings that tend to generate a high volume of unresolved follow-ups.
