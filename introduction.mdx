## What ConnectMeetings does

ConnectMeetings covers every stage of your meeting lifecycle — from the first scheduling request through to follow-up on action items.

- **Smart scheduling** — Find the best meeting time across teams by checking real-time calendar availability.
- **Collaborative agenda builder** — Create structured agendas using templates, assign items to presenters, and route agendas through an approval workflow before they go out.
- **Live meeting minutes** — Capture minutes in real time, link decisions directly to agenda items, and auto-generate a formatted minutes document when the meeting closes.
- **Action item tracking** — Assign tasks with owners and due dates during the meeting, then track completion rates on your dashboard.
- **Automated reminders** — Send email reminders to attendees before every meeting and before action item deadlines. SMS reminders are available on the Enterprise plan.
- **Microsoft 365 deep integration** — Sync with Outlook calendars, launch meetings in Teams, and archive minutes and documents to SharePoint automatically.
- **Meeting analytics** — Monitor attendance rates, average meeting duration, and action item completion rates across your organization.
- **Formal governance workflows** — Record motions, votes, and roll calls to meet the requirements of public-sector governance and procedural bylaws.
- **Enterprise-grade security** — SOC 2 Type II certified, AES-256 encryption at rest, and TLS 1.3 in transit. Enterprise customers can also elect Canadian data residency.

Trusted by 2,400+ organizations with a 98% uptime SLA, ConnectMeetings is purpose-built for the reliability and transparency that public-sector work demands.

## Get started in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up for ConnectMeetings">
    Go to [connectmeetings.ca](https://connectmeetings.ca) and click **Sign Up**. Enter your organization details and choose the plan that fits your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Microsoft 365">
    Authorize the Outlook and Teams integration so ConnectMeetings can sync calendars and send meeting invitations on your behalf.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first meeting">
    Click **New Meeting**, set a title, date, and time, then invite your attendees. ConnectMeetings checks everyone's availability automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Add your colleagues with the role that matches their responsibilities — Admin, Organizer, Participant, or View Only. They will receive an email invitation and can join your organization with one click.
  </Step>
</Steps>
