Nearly every Connect event follows the same three-beat structure. Internalise the rhythm and use the calmer moments as much as the appointment blocks.

## The shape of a Connect event

<Steps>
  <Step title="Day one: arrive and open">
    Regiception (combined registration and reception), a first-timer orientation at flagships, a welcome education session, the first block of marketplace appointments, and an opening reception hosted by the destination.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Day two: the working day">
    Breakfast and education, a morning appointment block, lunch and learn, an afternoon appointment block, then an open night in the host city. This is where most of the business gets done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Day three: close out">
    Grab-and-go breakfast, a final appointment block, and departures. Flagships add a closing concert or the annual Connect 5K.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Regional events compress this into days one and two. Flagships extend it with tradeshow experiences, speaker showcases, hosted meals, and a rebooking lounge.

## Working the appointment blocks

<Info>
  Flagship appointment blocks run **10:00-12:00 and 15:00-17:00** on days two and three. Regional blocks are similar in shape but shorter overall.
</Info>

- **Arrive early** at your table or booth to reset between appointments
- **Keep to the clock**. Appointments are short; running long punishes the next buyer, not just you
- **Log outcomes** immediately after each meeting. A rating and a two-line note is enough; you will forget by dinner otherwise
- **Watch for no-shows** and report them to the concierge desk so the buyer's schedule can be adjusted and their deposit compliance tracked

## Between appointment blocks

The soft moments matter as much as the hard schedule:

- **Hosted breakfasts and lunches** are curated by track and are the best places for extended conversations with buyers who would otherwise be capped at a short appointment
- **Opening receptions and open nights** are usually hosted by the destination and are excellent for informal introductions
- **Education sessions** are where you meet buyers who are not on your schedule but are in your market
- **The Connect 5K, morning walk clubs, and wellness activations** are small-group settings that build memorable rapport

## Rebooking onsite

Flagships run a **dedicated rebooking lounge** where returning suppliers can commit to next season before leaving. If your organisation has approval to renew, do it onsite. Renewals close at a materially higher rate and larger deal size than net new, and rebooking momentum onsite is part of why.

<Tip>
  Bring a signing authority or at minimum a rate sheet to the rebooking lounge. Deals not closed onsite regularly slip a full quarter before they land.
</Tip>

## Onsite support

- **Concierge / help desk**: schedule changes, badge issues, appointment substitutions
- **Registration**: badge pickup, walk-up power pass upgrades
- **Cancellations mid-event**: `connect.cancellations@informa.com`
