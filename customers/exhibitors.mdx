Connect Meetings runs marketplace-style events where hospitality suppliers meet pre-qualified event planners in pre-scheduled, one-to-one appointments. This guide is for suppliers evaluating or preparing for a Connect event.

<Info>
  Connect Meetings is the trade show business inside **Informa Connect**, a division of Informa PLC. See [Who we are](/company/overview) for how the business is structured.
</Info>

## Who this guide is for

Hospitality suppliers exhibiting at Connect marketplace events, including:

- Convention and visitors bureaus (CVBs) and destination marketing organisations (DMOs)
- Hotels, resorts, and convention centres
- Audiovisual, production, and event technology vendors
- Airlines, ground transportation, and gifting or experiential partners

If you are an event planner considering attending as a sponsored guest, use the [Hosted Buyer Guide](/customers/hosted-buyers) instead.

## How a Connect marketplace works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Qualified buyers are recruited">
    Event planners apply to the hosted buyer programme and are vetted for scale, sourcing authority, and event history. See [Business model](/company/business-model) for the full qualification bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Suppliers buy appointment packages">
    You purchase a package priced by number of one-to-one appointments with those hosted buyers. Regional events start at \$4,250 for 12 appointments; the flagships offer higher tiers with more appointments and preferred placement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Appointments are matched in advance">
    Requests are collected from both sides and matched, often AI-assisted, before anyone travels. You arrive with a schedule, not a hope.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You meet buyers on the marketplace floor">
    Appointment blocks run in fixed windows across the event days, alongside education, networking, and hosted meals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You rebook onsite">
    A dedicated rebooking lounge at the flagship lets returning suppliers commit to the next event before leaving. Renewals are the core of the business.
  </Step>
</Steps>
