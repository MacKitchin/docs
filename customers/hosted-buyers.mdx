Connect Meetings runs marketplace-style events where qualified event planners attend on a sponsored basis and meet hospitality suppliers in pre-scheduled appointments. This guide is for planners considering the hosted buyer programme.

<Info>
  Connect Meetings is the trade show business inside **Informa Connect**, a division of Informa PLC. See [Who we are](/company/overview) for how the business is structured.
</Info>

## What sponsored attendance means

As a hosted buyer, Connect covers the cost of attending in exchange for a **committed appointment schedule with suppliers**. In practical terms:

- Roundtrip airfare, hotel for the event nights, and meals are covered
- All networking events and the education programme are included
- You take a full schedule of one-to-one appointments with suppliers matched to your sourcing needs
- A **\$200 refundable deposit** is held and returned when you complete the assigned appointments

<Warning>
  The deposit is the enforcement mechanism for the programme. Missing appointments forfeits the deposit and affects your eligibility for future events.
</Warning>

## Who this guide is for

Event planners with real sourcing authority for group business:

- **Corporate** meeting and event planners
- **Association** meeting professionals
- **Sports** event organisers
- **Specialty** planners: social, military, and fraternal groups
- **Faith-based**, multicultural, LGBTQ\+, and women's group planners
- **International (RTO)** receptive tour operators booking inbound North American travel
- **Business travel** buyers with meaningful travel-management budgets

If you sell services into the events industry, use the [Exhibitor Guide](/customers/exhibitors) instead.

## The baseline bar

Across most tracks and events, the general qualification is:

- **50 or more rooms on peak nights** for the events you source
- Ability to **rotate destinations** nationally or regionally
- A **two-year verifiable event history**

Some tracks stack additional requirements on top - budget size, seniority, or event scale. Full detail is on [Application and qualification](/customers/buyer-qualification).
