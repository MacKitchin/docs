A good marketplace day starts with a good pre-event week. Use this as a checklist.

## Sourcing brief

Before you request suppliers, write down what you are actually sourcing. This shapes your appointment schedule.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Events on the roadmap">
    Which events do you need to source in the next 12 to 24 months? Dates, room block sizes, meeting space requirements, budget range.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Destination profile">
    First-tier city, second-tier city, resort, urban, warm-weather, drive market, direct flights required, accessibility considerations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Venue and service needs">
    Convention centre versus hotel-anchored, AV and production, unique venues for offsite events, F&B expectations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Concessions that move the deal">
    What matters most: rate, complimentary rooms, meeting space, F&B credit, attrition, cancellation flexibility.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Building your request list

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the event platform">
    Once approved, you get access to the attendee directory and event app with the full supplier list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by supplier category">
    Use category filters (destinations, hotels, venues, event tech, etc.) to focus.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prioritise mutual fit">
    Suppliers marked as sponsors, hosts, or in your track are more likely to match. Blue-sky requests are fine but low probability.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit requests before the deadline">
    Requests submitted after the deadline are matched against residual supply. Get yours in early.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Aim for a request list of roughly **1.5x to 2x your appointment count**. Some requests will be unmatched (no supplier interest, capacity, or fit) and the buffer lets the matching engine build a full schedule.
</Tip>

## Travel arrangements

- Your paired Hosted Buyer Experience contact will confirm the booking process for flights and the hotel block
- Book **within the published window and policy** to avoid out-of-pocket costs
- Coordinate **ground transportation** from your home airport and any pre- or post-event stays
- Confirm **arrival and departure timing** against day-one Regiception and day-three final appointment blocks

## Pre-event admin

- **Complete your profile** in the event platform so suppliers can find you
- **Upload a headshot** if requested; used in the app and directory
- **Register for optional sessions**: MPAC training, walk clubs, hosted excursions where offered
- **Note the code of conduct** and any dress guidance
- **Save the concierge and cancellations emails** in case anything changes late

## What to bring

- **Sourcing brief** in a format you can quickly show a supplier: dates, sizes, must-haves
- **A method for capturing notes** during appointments. The event app usually supports rating and note-taking; keep a backup
- **Business cards or digital equivalent**
- **Comfortable shoes**; you will walk more than you expect between appointment rooms and networking spaces
- **A charger and battery pack** for long days on the show floor

## Communicating with suppliers before the event

Some suppliers will reach out ahead of the event with intro emails or pre-read materials. This is normal and helpful. You are not obliged to reply to every one, but responding to key targets ahead of time sharpens the appointment when it happens.

<Warning>
  Do not commit to specific dates, room blocks, or contract terms in pre-event outreach. Use the appointment to have that conversation properly, in person.
</Warning>
