## Special cases

### International (RTO) buyers

International buyers attending the flagship as receptive tour operators receive a **flight stipend up to \$600 per company** rather than direct flight booking. At Spring Marketplace, qualified RTOs receive complimentary hosted buyer registration.

### Informa colleagues

Informa PLC colleagues attending under the Informa track have registration fees waived but travel and hotel arrangements follow standard Informa T&E policy, not the hosted buyer package.

### Regional versus flagship

Coverage is broadly the same across regional and flagship events, but the shape differs:

| Item | Flagship | Regional |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hotel nights covered | Typically **3 nights** | Typically **2 nights** |
| Education content | Up to 14 to 15 hours CMP-credit | 3\+ hours |
| Appointments | Up to 45 (flagship) | Up to 25 (typical regional) |
| Signature moments | Multiple, including closing concert or Connect 5K | Destination-hosted opening reception, open night |

### Club Connect

Some regional events run **Club Connect**, a points-based hosted buyer rewards programme. Points can be redeemed for perks such as priority seating, hospitality suite access, and airport-hotel transportation. Ask your Hosted Buyer Experience contact whether the event you are attending includes it.

## Cancellation and its cost

<Warning>
  Hosted buyer cancellation windows and refund rules are published differently across event sites. Always confirm the specific cutoff and any late-cancel fee for the event you are attending before booking non-refundable travel or hotel extensions.
</Warning>

As a general reference:

- **30 or more days** before the event: full refund or credit
- **Inside 7 days** of the event: no refund plus a one-night hotel fee
- **Deposit** returned on completing the appointment schedule; forfeited on no-shows
