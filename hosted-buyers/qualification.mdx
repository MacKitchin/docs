Applying is short. Qualification is real. This page walks through both.

## The application

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit the application">
    The application takes about **ten minutes** to complete. You provide company details, role, event portfolio, sourcing scale, and preferred track.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay the refundable deposit">
    A **\$200 refundable deposit** secures your application. It is returned when you complete the assigned appointment schedule at the event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hosted Buyer Experience Team review">
    The team vets your application against track criteria: role, sourcing volume, event history, rotation capacity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pairing with a dedicated contact">
    Approved hosted buyers are paired with a dedicated point of contact from the Hosted Buyer Experience Team who supports you through pre-event, onsite, and follow-up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Connect verifies

- **Role** and decision authority for sourcing
- **Event portfolio**: two-year verifiable history
- **Sourcing scale**: rooms on peak, budget size, or attendance depending on track
- **Rotation**: national or regional as appropriate to the event

Providing accurate information is important. Misrepresenting scale or authority is grounds for disqualification and can affect future eligibility across the Connect portfolio.

## The deposit

<Info>
  The \$200 deposit is **refundable in full** when you complete the appointment schedule assigned to you. It is not an event fee.
</Info>

The deposit is the mechanism that keeps the marketplace commercially viable. Suppliers pay for a specific number of appointments, and those appointments only have value if hosted buyers actually show up. From the [business model page](/company/business-model):

> The deposit is the enforcement mechanism for the whole model. If hosted buyers skip appointments, suppliers do not renew, and renewals are 79 percent of supplier revenue.

### When the deposit is forfeited

- **No-shows** at scheduled appointments
- **Late cancellation** inside the hosted buyer cancellation window (typically inside 7 days of the event, plus a one-night hotel fee)
- **Failure to complete** the assigned appointment schedule

<Warning>
  Two hosted buyer cancellation policies are published across the portfolio (one on the Hosted Buyers page, one in the Terms & Conditions). Always confirm the cutoff for the specific event you are attending before assuming the general 30-day cutoff applies.
</Warning>

### When the deposit is returned

- **Full refund or credit** if you cancel **30 or more days** before the event
- **Full refund** on completing your assigned appointment schedule onsite

## The appointment commitment

As a hosted buyer you commit to a **full appointment schedule** matched to your sourcing needs. At the flagship this can be **up to 45 appointments** across the event. At regional events it is usually shorter.

Appointments are:

- **One-to-one** with a supplier representative
- **Short and focused** (some events describe them as nine minutes)
- **Matched in advance** from mutual-interest requests, often AI-assisted
- **Tracked onsite**; missed appointments count against your deposit

See [Onsite experience](/hosted-buyers/onsite) for how the day is structured around these blocks.

## After approval

Once approved and paired with your Hosted Buyer Experience contact, you move into the pre-event phase: submitting supplier requests, arranging travel, and preparing your sourcing brief. See [Preparing to attend](/hosted-buyers/prepare).
