The event ends but the sourcing work continues for weeks after. This page covers what happens right away and what to plan for the next season.

## Deposit return

Your \$200 deposit is returned when you have **completed the appointment schedule** assigned to you.

<Info>
  Attendance is tracked at each appointment via the check-in process. If a supplier no-showed, the concierge desk logs it so it does not count against you.
</Info>

If you believe your deposit has been forfeited in error, contact `connect.cancellations@informa.com` with the event name and your registration details.

## Supplier follow-up

Suppliers will typically reach out to you within a few days of the event with tailored proposals based on your appointment conversation. To keep sourcing moving:

- **Reply to the ones you are progressing** even briefly, so they know where to focus
- **Politely decline** the ones you know are not a fit, so both sides can move on
- **Route serious proposals** into your normal sourcing workflow
- **Save appointment notes** somewhere you will find them next time you source that destination or venue type

## Post-event feedback

Connect sends a **post-event survey**. Complete it. Feedback directly shapes:

- Which suppliers appear at future events
- Track composition and qualification bars
- Matching rules for next season's appointments
- Programme design and hosted amenities

## Applying for the next event

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the season calendar">
    See the full [Event calendar](/events/overview) to pick your next Connect event based on geography, track, and sourcing timing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply early">
    Hosted buyer capacity is capped per track. Applying early improves your odds of a slot in your first-choice event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your profile">
    Refresh your event portfolio, sourcing volume, and role so the Hosted Buyer Experience Team can vet you against current criteria.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Signal continuity">
    Returning hosted buyers with a strong attendance record are prioritised. Your track record from this event carries into the next application.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What returning attendance looks like

- Faster application review since your history is on file
- Priority consideration for oversubscribed tracks
- Continued relationship with your Hosted Buyer Experience contact where possible
- Eligibility for programmes like Club Connect where they apply
