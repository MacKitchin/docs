Connect Travel is the travel-sector brand within Connect Meetings & Events. Its documented live-event products are **[eTourism Marketplace](https://informaconnect.com/etourism-marketplace/)** and **[eTourism Summit](https://informaconnect.com/etourism-summit/)**. Portfolio hub: [Events, Travel and Sports](https://informaconnect.com/events-travel-sports/). Internal event facts: [Connect Travel events](/events/connect-travel). Public URLs: [Public event sites](/events/public-sites).

## Distinction from the Connect marketplace portfolio

| Connect marketplace | Connect Travel |
| --- | --- |
| Recruits association, corporate, sports, specialty, and related event planners | Recruits destination-marketing professionals and related travel-industry audiences |
| Suppliers sell destinations, venues, hospitality, and event services | Suppliers serve destination-marketing and tourism needs |
| Core format is the reverse-tradeshow appointment marketplace | Portfolio includes both a hosted marketplace and a paid summit |
