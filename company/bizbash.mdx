BizBash is the events-media brand inside Connect Meetings & Events. The public site is [bizbash.com](https://www.bizbash.com). Captured **12 August 2026**.

<Info>
  Founded in **2000**. Acquired by Connect Meetings in **2020**. VP BizBash: Michela Giovannotto. Sales inquiries published as `bizbash.sales@informa.com`.
</Info>

Company Brain previously treated BizBash as a one-line media partner plus a 2025 rate card. The website is a full product: daily editorial, two print issues a year, a recognition calendar, webinars, a supplier directory, and its own live events. Rate-card dollars stay on [Media and amplification](/products-services/media-amplification). This page is the brand.

## What the site is

Daily digital coverage for meeting and event professionals. The About page describes the audience as planners, vendors, suppliers, business owners, PR professionals, and aspiring industry leaders. Editorial verticals on the live nav:

| Vertical | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Event production and planning | Experiential marketing, fabrication, programming, rentals, corporate events, meetings, trade shows |
| Event design | Décor, corporate swag |
| Event catering | Food and beverage trends |
| Event tech | AI for meetings, tools, virtual events |
| Event venues | Hotels and new-venue guides by city |
| Sporting events | Brand activations and sports-event coverage |

That last vertical is why Sports Innovation Forum and The Women's Summit sit on the BizBash side of the business.

## What not to create from this site

- A page per editorial vertical. Those are a CMS taxonomy, not products.
- A page that restates the media kits. That already lives under [Media and amplification](/products-services/media-amplification).
- Record-level directory listings or named advertiser accounts.
