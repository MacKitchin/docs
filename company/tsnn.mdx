TSNN is **Trade Show News Network**, the exhibition-industry news brand inside Connect Meetings & Events. The public site is [tsnn.com](https://www.tsnn.com/). Captured **12 August 2026**.

<Info>
  About Us: world's leading news and online resource for the trade show, exhibition and event industry **since 1996**. Owned by Informa. Longest-running media brand dedicated to the trade show industry. Group content: Danica Tormohlen, VP of Group Content.
</Info>

Company Brain previously treated TSNN as a one-line media partner plus a 2025 rate card. The website is a full news product: daily editorial, a newsletter suite, a Top 250 ranking, annual awards, a trade-show calendar, podcasts, and an advertiser kit that is still labelled **2025**. Rate-card dollars stay on [Media & amplification](/products-services/media-amplification). This page is the brand.

## What not to create from this site

- A page per editorial vertical. Those are a CMS taxonomy.
- A page that restates the 2025 media kit. That already lives under Products.
- The Top 250 as a ranked table of named shows. That is third-party event data, not our calendar.
- Record-level advertiser accounts.
