## How brands appear in the data

Brand is not a single field. It surfaces in three places, and mixing them up is a common analysis error.

| Field | Object | Values | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Organization__c` | OpportunityLineItem | Meetings, Travel, BizBash, TSNN | Splitting revenue by business unit. This is the reliable one. |
| `Product_Type__c` | OpportunityLineItem | Association, Sports, BizBash, Marketplace, Webinar, and 50\+ others | Product-level mix |
| `Event_Name__c` | OpportunityLineItem | Spring Marketplace, MEGA, Winter Marketplace, and others | Attributing revenue to a specific event |

```sql Revenue by brand for a product year
SELECT Organization__c, SUM(TotalPrice) revenue
FROM OpportunityLineItem
WHERE Opportunity.StageName = 'Closed Won'
  AND Product_Year__c = '2026'
GROUP BY Organization__c
```

<Warning>
  Connect and BizBash grew up as separate businesses and their account, contact, and lead data has not been fully merged. As of the current analysis there are 26 account-team rep assignments covering 20,876 advertiser accounts on the BizBash side. That gap is the whole point of the [data consolidation project](/projects/data-consolidation). Do not assume BizBash advertiser accounts have an owner.
</Warning>

## Media partnerships across events

BizBash, TSNN, and Corporate Event News appear as media partners on most Connect event sites. They are internal brands acting as media partners to our own events, which is intentional but occasionally confuses external readers of a sponsor list.
