Revenue is booked through event packages, sponsorships, and media. Most of it comes from existing accounts.

## Revenue engines

Connect Meetings & Events earns revenue through event appointment packages, sponsorships, media and advertising, event amplification, and related registration or service lines. The detailed product categories live on [Business and revenue model](/company/business-model).

## Why renewals matter

The current `connect-prod` analysis shows that Existing Business converts at a materially higher rate and larger average value than New Business. These are point-in-time CRM findings, not permanent targets.

| Opportunity type | Observed win rate | Observed average |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing Business | 84.6% | Approximately \$18.8K |
| New Business | 67.2% | Approximately \$10.1K |

<Tip>
  Rebooking is a core commercial workflow. It is not merely an onsite convenience.
</Tip>

## Salesforce representation

- `Opportunity.Type` distinguishes **New Business** and **Existing Business**.
- `Product_Year__c` is the year the product is for, not necessarily the close-date year.
- `OpportunityLineItem.Organization__c` is the reliable business-unit split.
- Cash reporting excludes `Barter__c = true`.
- Historical analysis must account for edits after Closed Won.

Use [Metrics and definitions](/operations/data/metrics) for current query definitions and [Sales process](/company/sales-process) for lifecycle detail.

<Warning>
  Do not quote these aggregate findings as a forecast for a named account or event. Query the live org for current operational decisions.
</Warning>
