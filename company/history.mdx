This page records only milestones supported by the current source set. It is not a complete corporate history.

## Confirmed milestones

| Year | Milestone | Source status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1996** | TSNN describes itself as serving the trade-show industry since 1996. | Public TSNN About page, captured 12 August 2026 |
| **2000** | BizBash was founded. | Public BizBash About page, captured 12 August 2026 |
| **2020** | Connect Meetings acquired BizBash. | Public BizBash About page |
| **2026** | Connect Meetings & Events operates inside Informa Connect with four documented brands: Connect, Connect Travel, BizBash, and TSNN. | Company OS, public brand sites, and `connect-prod` structure |

## What remains undocumented

The current source set does not establish a reliable founding timeline for the Connect marketplace business, the complete acquisition history of TSNN, or every prior business-unit name.

  Do not fill this gap from memory or marketing copy. Add a milestone only after confirming it with an owned corporate source and recording that source here.
