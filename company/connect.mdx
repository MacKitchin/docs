Connect is the core appointment-event brand within Connect Meetings & Events. It operates the flagship and regional marketplace portfolio documented on this site.

## What the brand does

- Recruits and qualifies event planners as hosted buyers.
- Sells appointment packages and sponsorships to hospitality and event-industry suppliers.
- Matches buyer sourcing needs to supplier capabilities before the event.
- Runs education, networking, marketplace appointments, and onsite rebooking.
