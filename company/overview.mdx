## Where we sit

**Informa PLC** is the parent group. **Informa Connect** is the division. **Connect Meetings & Events** is our business unit inside that division, and it is what this Company Brain documents.

The reporting line into the group runs through **Andrew Mullins**, an Informa executive external to Connect, to **Matt Johnson**, Managing Director, who owns Connect Meetings & Events end to end.

<Info>
  When an event site says "operated by Informa Connect Division of Informa PLC", that is us. Customer-facing material uses the **Connect** brand; internal systems and email use `@informa.com`.
</Info>

## Customers

The sales model is **supplier-side**. We sell to the people who want to be found, and we recruit the people doing the finding.

| Side | Who they are | What they do with us |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Suppliers** (paying customers) | CVBs and DMOs, hotels and resorts, convention centres and venues, AV and production companies, event technology vendors, airlines, gifting and merchandise companies, experiential agencies | Buy appointment packages, sponsorships, booths, and advertising |
| **Buyers** (recruited, mostly sponsored) | Association, corporate, sports, specialty, and international event planners | Attend as hosted buyers, take pre-scheduled appointments, source destinations and services |

In Salesforce both sides live on the `Account` object and are separated by `Type` and `Primary_Category__c`. Supplier accounts are identified by `Primary_Category__c LIKE 'Supplier-%'` - roughly 13,800 accounts across 29 supplier categories.

<Tip>
  The renewal numbers are the single most load-bearing fact about this business: existing business converts at a materially higher rate and at nearly twice the deal size. Rebooking is the revenue engine, not new logo acquisition.
</Tip>
