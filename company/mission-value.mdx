## The value exchange

| Audience | Value received | Commitment or commercial exchange |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Suppliers** | Pre-scheduled access to qualified event planners, plus optional sponsorship and media reach | Purchase appointment packages, sponsorships, or advertising |
| **Hosted buyers** | Event access, qualified supplier discovery, education, networking, and published travel or hotel coverage | Meet the applicable eligibility criteria and complete the assigned appointment schedule |
| **Staff** | A repeatable commercial and operating model across events, media, and brands | Protect source accuracy, customer commitments, data governance, and escalation boundaries |

## What differentiates the model

- Appointments are matched and scheduled before the event rather than left to floor traffic.
- Hosted-buyer qualification protects the value of the supplier appointment package.
- Events, media, sponsorships, and amplification provide complementary commercial routes.
- Rebooking and existing business convert at materially higher rates than new-logo acquisition.

<Tip>
  The most important operating principle is alignment: qualified buyers attend their appointments, suppliers receive the access sold to them, and staff communicate only verified terms.
</Tip>
