## What we sell, and to whom

The model is **supplier-side**. We sell to hospitality and travel suppliers. We recruit planners as hosted buyers. See [Business model](/company/business-model).

In Salesforce a deal is an **Opportunity**. Every Opportunity carries `IO_Number__c` (Insertion Order). Product detail lives on `OpportunityLineItem`, not on the Opportunity header. `Opportunity_Product_Type__c` is **100% null** — do not analyse it.

Brand / business unit on the line: `Organization__c` = Meetings, Travel, BizBash, TSNN. Product category on the line: Digital, Event, Event Amplification, Special Projects.

## Two processes, one picklist

Record types are used sparingly. Differentiation is mostly picklists. The `StageName` picklist holds **two merged sales processes** that share terminal stages.

| Process (as inferred in the Salesforce KB) | Open stages | Shared close |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Event / marketplace | Prospecting → Interested → Proposal → Verbal | Closed Won, Closed Lost, Expired |
| Media / advertising | Made Contact & Set Meeting → Proposal Creation (BANT Known) → Proposal Sent → Negotiation | Closed Won, Closed Lost, Expired |

Stages that sit across both and matter commercially:

| Stage | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Previous Year Purchase** | Renewal pipeline. Existing suppliers being worked for the next product year. |
| **MSA Signed / SOW Signed** | Contract-focused, typically larger deals. |
| **Closed Won** | Booked. `IsClosed = true`, `IsWon = true`. |
| **Expired** | Timed out without a decision. Closed but **not** lost. Exclude from win-rate denominators. |
| **Closed Lost** | Lost. `Reason_Lost__c` is blank on 421 of 424 lost deals — do not expect a structured reason. |

The full stage ladder, including `IsClosed` / `IsWon`, is on the [data model](/operations/data/data-model).

<Tip>
  `Opportunity.Type` — **New Business** vs **Existing Business** — is the most predictive commercial field in the org. Renewals win at 84.6% and average around \$18.8K; new logos win at 67.2% and average around \$10.1K. Rebooking is the engine.
</Tip>

## How a deal is built

<Steps>
  <Step title="Account and contact already exist, or are created">
    Supplier accounts: `Primary_Category__c LIKE 'Supplier-%'`. Territory is derived from `BillingState`, not a territory object. Do not assume a BizBash advertiser account has an owner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Opportunity is opened with an IO number">
    `IO_Number__c` is required and unique. `CloseDate` is required. Set `Type` (New vs Existing) correctly — it drives every renewal analysis.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Products go on OpportunityLineItem">
    Appointment packages, sponsorships, media, amplification. `Product_Year__c` is the year the product is *for*, not the close-date year. A December 2026 close can be product year 2027.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agreement is sent in DocuSign">
    From the Customer FAQ library: agreements are sent and signed via DocuSign; once signed, participation or the campaign is treated as secured and fulfilment begins. After signature, suppliers often receive an "Action Required: Attendee Details" DocuSign for who is attending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Media and digital orders may enter AdBook">
    Not every opportunity uses AdBook. Check `AB2__Order_Created__c = true` before relying on any `AB2__` field. Cash at line level is `AB2__ABPayType__c = 'PaidFor'`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Closed Won, then billing">
    Salesforce Opportunities are the source of booked revenue. FinancialForce holds invoices with limited field access — prefer Opportunity billing fields (`Billing_Terms__c`, `Invoice_Number__c`, `Opportunity_Paid_In_Full__c`). Customer-facing payment questions: see the FAQ on invoices (BizBashPayNow / Square). Do not quote a specific amount or schedule.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After Closed Won

`Amount` can be edited after the deal is won. For a historically accurate figure check `Change_Made_After_Closed_Won__c` and `Initial_Close_Won_Date__c`.

Cash revenue excludes `Barter__c = true`. Default reporting filters: `Account_Status__c != 'Inactive'`, `StageName NOT IN ('Expired', 'Closed Lost')`, `IsDeleted = false`.

## Renewals and rebooking

Existing Business is worked from **Previous Year Purchase** and from onsite rebooking. The business-model page records a dedicated rebooking lounge at the event. There is no separate "renewal object" — it is the same Opportunity model with `Type = Existing Business` and the next `Product_Year__c`.

Use Account spend rollups (`Total_Spend_This_Product_Year__c` and the five prior years) rather than re-aggregating opportunities. The `dlrs` package maintains them.
