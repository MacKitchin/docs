`connect-prod` is a heavily integrated org. Thirteen managed packages sit on top of it, several with triggers on core objects. Understanding which package owns which field prefix is the difference between a correct query and a confusing one.

## The critical two

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="AdBook (AB2)" icon="newspaper">
    Full ad order management: line items, rate cards, pricing, and the IO lifecycle. 25\+ objects, 200\+ Apex classes, triggers on Account, Contact, Opportunity, OpportunityLineItem, and Product2. Every `AB2__` field comes from here.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Visit Create" icon="ticket">
    The hosted buyer registration platform. Feeds `Registration__c` via webhook, logged in `Visit_Webhook_Log__c`. Keys are `Visit_Registration_Key__c`, `Visit_Visitor_Id__c`, `Visit_Order_Id__c`, and `Visit_Expo_Id__c`. Over 40 registration fields originate from Visit forms.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Working with AdBook fields

| On Opportunity | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `AB2__ABOrderID__c` | AdBook order ID |
| `AB2__AB_IO_Number__c` | AdBook IO number |
| `AB2__ABTotalGrossCost__c` | Total gross cost |
| `AB2__ABBillTo__c` | Bill to Client or Agency |
| `AB2__ABBillType__c` | Net or Gross |
| `AB2__ABAgencyCommission__c` | Agency commission percentage |
| `AB2__Order_Created__c` | Whether an order exists in AdBook |
| `AB2__ABDoNotSync__c` | Exclude from sync |

| On OpportunityLineItem | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `AB2__ABDropID__c` | Ad drop or placement ID |
| `AB2__ABDropCost__c` / `AB2__ABDropPrice__c` | Cost and price |
| `AB2__ABPayType__c` | **PaidFor, ValueAdd, NonBillable, Barter, Promo** |
| `AB2__ABPriceType__c` | CPM, CPC, Flat Rate, CPZ |
| `AB2__ABImpressions__c` | Contracted impressions |
| `AB2__ABDropCancelled__c` | Whether the drop was cancelled |

<Warning>
  Not every opportunity uses AdBook. Check `AB2__Order_Created__c = true` before relying on any `AB2__` field, or your averages will be computed over nulls. And for cash revenue at line level, filter `AB2__ABPayType__c = 'PaidFor'` - ValueAdd, NonBillable, Barter, and Promo are all non-cash.
</Warning>

## Billing and payment fields

Revenue flows through three systems, and the boundaries matter.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Salesforce Opportunities are the source of truth for booked revenue">
    `Billing_Terms__c` (One Time, Recurring, Compound, Cancelled, Billed in Full), `Billing_Start_Date__c`, `Invoice_Number__c`, `Invoice_Date__c`, `Opportunity_Paid_In_Full__c`, `PO_Number__c`, `Opportunity_Currency__c`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AdBook manages digital ad orders and syncs back">
    The IO lifecycle lives in AdBook and reflects into `AB2__` fields on the opportunity and its line items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="FinancialForce handles invoicing">
    Invoice objects exist but with limited field access. For detailed invoice analysis, use Opportunity-level billing fields unless additional FinancialForce field access has been granted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Line-level payment fields include `AR_Payment_Status__c`, `Paid_In_Full_Date__c`, `Do_Not_Invoice__c`, and `Delayed_Invoice_Date__c`.

<Warning>
  **Currency.** Most deals are USD but some are CAD, flagged on `Opportunity_Currency__c`. Do not sum across currencies without converting.
</Warning>

## Technical debt worth knowing about

- **Two agency-contact objects.** `Agency_Contact__c` and `Agency_Contacts__c` both exist and appear to overlap.
- **`ProposalDetailsTrigger`** on `Proposal_Details__c` runs on API version 29, dating from 2014.
- **`Order_Details__c`** is likely legacy or supplementary to `OpportunityLineItem`.
- **Silverpop and Marketing Cloud coexist**, with Silverpop notionally being retired.
