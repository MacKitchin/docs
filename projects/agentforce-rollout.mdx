## Overview

Stand up and operationalise the Agentforce agent roster in `connect-prod` - Connect (SF Agent), Data Steward, Flow Automation, Notebook AI, Opportunity Agent, and Agentforce Sales Coach - prioritised by the [automation roadmap](/ai/automation-roadmap), which is worth roughly 1,405 hours a year.

## Goals and success criteria

- **P1 workflows live**: FAQ deflection (250 hrs/yr), campaign analytics and reporting (300 hrs/yr), attendee-details collection and follow-up (200 hrs/yr).
- **Every agent documented** in the AI & Agent Library with an owner and a status.
- **Customer-facing answers grounded** in the Customer FAQs library or the Live Events wiki, with citations.

See [Agent roster](/ai/agents) for what each one does. All six are recorded as Active in the AI & Agent Library, all on the Salesforce platform, all owned by Mac Kitchin.

## Risks and blockers

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Agents answering from stale event facts | Wrong customer answers, at scale | Chatbots restricted to the Live Events wiki; refresh SOP in place | **Controlled** |

<Warning>
  The stale-event-facts risk is not theoretical. Multiple event sites currently publish contradictory dates, hosts, venues, and cancellation policies - see [Known issues](/governance/known-issues). An agent with unrestricted retrieval would confidently give a supplier the wrong date for Connect North. Restricting customer-facing retrieval to the Live Events wiki is the entire mitigation.
</Warning>

## How the three P1 workflows work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="FAQ deflection - 250 hrs/yr" icon="message-circle-question">
    The two highest-frequency supplier questions are "what attendee details do I submit after registering?" and "when and where is Connect Marketplace 2026?", both with very high frequency signal and confidence of 0.86 and 0.90. Both are pure lookups. The agent answers from Customer FAQs and the Live Events wiki, cites the source, and never improvises a date.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Campaign analytics and reporting - 300 hrs/yr" icon="chart-line">
    The largest single time sink. Suppliers ask for campaign performance and reps assemble it manually. Split into self-service reporting plus an agent that assembles and explains the numbers. Depends on reporting dashboards being indexed, which is currently marked **Working** rather than canonical.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Attendee details collection and follow-up - 200 hrs/yr" icon="users">
    After a supplier registers, someone has to chase attendee details. This is the number-one question by frequency and it is entirely mechanical - collect, validate, remind, escalate on non-response.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Evaluation and guardrails

The answer-style guide distilled from the highest-performing reps, and the hard limits, are documented in full on [Guardrails](/ai/guardrails). In short: lead with the value frame, be concrete about logistics, use honest urgency, never invent an offer, never quote a rate, never share a contact list, always propose a concrete next step, always cite.
