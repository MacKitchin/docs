## Overview

Consolidate three competing front doors and roughly 35 scattered databases into **Company OS**: PARA navigation over a master-database engine, with a Knowledge Base, department pages, an AI & Automation Hub, and a Source of Truth Registry.

The design principle is a clean separation between two layers.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Navigation layer" icon="compass">
    Projects, Areas, Resources, Archive. Front doors that show linked views. Optimised for a human finding something.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Data layer" icon="database">
    Master databases - People, Projects, Docs, Tasks, Areas, Resources. Each fact has exactly one home. Optimised for correctness.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Goals and success criteria

- One front door. Legacy hubs archived with redirect banners.
- Every domain has exactly one canonical home, recorded in the [source-of-truth registry](/governance/source-of-truth).
- A new hire can self-orient in under fifteen minutes.

## Key decisions

| Date | Decision | Why, and the trade-off | Decided by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Conventions the rebuild established

These now apply to anything added to the workspace, and by extension to this site.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Projects end, areas continue" icon="clock">
    If it has an end date and a definition of done, it is a project and belongs in Master Projects. If it is ongoing ownership, it is an area. Do not create area pages for time-bound work.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Link, do not copy" icon="link">
    Both projects and areas link to their canonical docs and sources rather than duplicating them. Duplication is what created the problem this project is solving.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="One canonical entry per information type" icon="badge-check">
    Every major information type gets exactly one Canonical registry entry, with an owner and a refresh cadence. When a source is replaced, the old row is marked Deprecated and linked to its replacement.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Database properties beat page bodies" icon="table">
    Owner, stage, priority, area, and timeline live in database properties and stay authoritative. Page bodies hold context and decisions, not status.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Why this matters beyond Notion

This project is the precondition for everything in the [AI stack](/ai/agents). An agent grounded on a workspace with three front doors, duplicate databases, and no truth levels will confidently cite the wrong thing. The registry is what lets an agent say "this source is marked Working, treat it with caution" instead of guessing.
