## Overview

Consolidate Salesforce Accounts, Contacts, Leads, Prospects, Opportunities, Campaigns, Campaign Members, and Account Team data across the Connect and BizBash businesses so that **routing, reporting, and sales coverage all work from one clean dataset**.

Connect and BizBash grew up separately. They now share a Salesforce org but not a coherent data model, and the seams show most clearly in account ownership.

## Goals and success criteria

- Account team coverage ready for BizBash routing.
- A deduplicated account and contact universe with categories preserved.
- The data-quality plan executed against the source analysis.

## The headline problem

<Warning>
  **26 account-team rep assignments against 20,876 advertiser accounts.** On the BizBash side, effectively no advertiser account has a recorded owner. Any routing rule, coverage report, or agent escalation path that assumes account ownership is currently operating on nothing.
</Warning>

## The second problem: loss data does not exist

<Warning>
  `Reason_Lost__c` is **blank on 421 of 424 lost deals**, and `Opportunity_Product_Type__c` is **100 percent null**. Structured win/loss and objection analytics are therefore impossible from CRM fields today. This is why the objection and loss intelligence in the [Supplier Knowledge Intelligence](/projects/supplier-knowledge) project had to be mined from email rather than queried.
</Warning>

## What the analysis established

Real numbers from supplier-account opportunities closing since 1 January 2025, filtered on `Account.Primary_Category__c LIKE 'Supplier-%'`.

| Metric | Value | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Closed Won | 492 deals, \$7.85M | Average won deal around \$16K |
| Closed Lost | 139 deals, \$1.93M | Roughly 78% win rate, won over won plus lost |
| Expired | 79 deals, \$0.66M | Roughly 69% win rate including expired - expiries are a follow-up gap, not a loss |
| Existing / renewal business | 84.6% win, $$6.22M, ~$$18.8K average | Renewals are the revenue engine |
| New business | 67.2% win, $$1.64M, ~$$10.1K average | New logos convert lower and smaller |
| Inbound vs outbound | 77.9% vs 71.5% win rate | Inbound converts better - prioritise inbound response time |

<Tip>
  The gap between the 78 percent and 69 percent win rates is 79 expired deals worth \$660K. Those are not deals we lost on merit; they are deals nobody followed up on. That is the cheapest revenue in the analysis.
</Tip>

## Why this blocks other work

Clean account ownership is a precondition for agent escalation (who does the agent hand off to?), for coverage reporting, and for any renewal-outreach automation. Given that renewals carry 79 percent of supplier revenue, an unowned account is an unrenewed account.
